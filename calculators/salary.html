Gross pay versus take-home pay

This converter calculates pay before taxes, benefits, retirement contributions or payroll deductions. For most wage earners, actual take-home pay depends on federal and state taxes, filing status, employer withholding and benefit choices. We intentionally do not present a gross-to-net estimate as an official tax calculation.

Comparing hourly and salaried offers

Convert two job offers to the same annual-gross basis. Remember to compare paid time off, unpaid time, bonuses, hours and benefits rather than looking only at salary numbers. A position advertised as hourly does not automatically guarantee a fixed number of hours each week.

How working weeks change the result

The work-week field is especially useful for part-year employment, seasonal positions and unpaid time off. Select your current pay period and edit hours and working weeks to match your situation.

Common questions

Is this a federal income tax calculator?

No, it shows gross earnings only, not withholding or net wages.

Does it include overtime?

No automatic overtime multiplier is applied. Enter your average equivalent hourly earnings or calculate overtime separately.
