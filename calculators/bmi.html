How do I figure BMI using pounds and inches?

Answer: Convert your height to inches (feet × 12, plus remaining inches), square that number, then divide your weight in pounds by the squared height and multiply by 703. The formula is BMI = weight (lb) ÷ [height (in)]² × 703, as documented by the CDC.

For example, an adult weighing 175 pounds at 5 feet 10 inches is 70 inches tall. The calculation is 175 ÷ (70 × 70) × 703 = approximately 25.1. The result is a screening value, not an assessment of body fat or an individual diagnosis.

What do adult BMI numbers mean?

For adults aged 20 or older, the CDC's adult categories are: below 18.5 (underweight), 18.5 to below 25 (healthy weight), 25 to below 30 (overweight), and 30 or higher (obesity). These thresholds are not the method used for children or teens. A BMI near a category boundary should not be interpreted as a precise medical finding.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. The CDC explains that BMI does not distinguish fat from muscle or bone and cannot show fat distribution. Clinicians may consider medical history, physical examination and other measures. If you are evaluating your own health, discuss the result with a qualified professional rather than making treatment decisions from BMI alone.

What BMI is

Body mass index is calculated from body weight and height. This tool uses US customary units (pounds and inches) and the adult BMI formula. It displays commonly used adult BMI classifications for general screening, not a measure of an individual's health or body composition.

Why BMI has important limitations

BMI cannot distinguish muscle mass from fat distribution and should be considered alongside age, medical history and other clinical indicators. It is not a substitute for a medical assessment and adult thresholds are not suitable for children or teens. The tool does not collect or store your measurements.

When to ask a professional

If you need health guidance or have concerns about weight, discuss appropriate assessments with a qualified clinician. Avoid using a single numeric result to make medical decisions.
