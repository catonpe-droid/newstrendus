How percentage discounts work

A 25% discount reduces the listed price by one quarter. The tool subtracts that discount before applying the optional sales-tax percentage. Some stores use different rules for multiple discounts, store credit, coupons or tax calculations—check their terms.

Compare actual savings

A discount label alone does not establish the lowest final price. Shipping, return fees, applicable tax and the seller's original reference price matter. Use this calculator with the store's actual checkout amounts.

Helpful shopping reminder

For multi-item orders, calculate item-specific discounts separately when eligible discounts or taxes differ. This is an estimate rather than a shopping platform integration.
