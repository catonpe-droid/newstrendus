Image transparency

Photo credits and image licenses

NewsTrendus uses a selection of genuine photographs supplied by independent photographers on Pexels. These images are used to represent broad editorial sections such as technology, business and sports, and are not presented as authentic photographs of the particular news event described beside them. A clear photographer credit and source link also appear directly below photographs on our news-brief pages.

Image usage and license

These images are available under the Pexels License, which allows free commercial website use, subject to its restrictions. The image creators retain their rights. We do not imply endorsement by the photographers, featured brands or identifiable persons. If permissions or availability change, we may replace a photograph with an original category illustration.
