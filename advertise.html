Frequently asked questions

Is this news coverage or paid advertising?

This is paid business promotion. Each accepted article and directory profile will be prominently labeled “Sponsored Advertising Content.” Sponsored outgoing links use the appropriate sponsored attribute. Payment does not influence independent news-source selection.

What happens after I submit?

Our team manually reviews your inquiry and replies by email. We do not automatically charge you or publish submissions.

Can I provide my own copy and pictures?

Yes, if they are accurate, original or properly licensed for this use. We may edit for clarity, accuracy, quality and publication standards, and will ask for approval before publishing the final sponsored profile.

Will this guarantee traffic, search ranking or backlinks?

No. This is an advertising placement, not a promise of audience size, SEO benefit, referral visits, leads, or search-engine indexing.

How long will my listing stay online?

We expect approved profiles to remain accessible while the relevant NewsTrendus offering operates, but there is no lifetime or perpetual hosting guarantee. Material errors, disputes, policy violations or site changes may require updates or removal.

When do I pay, and are refunds available?

Please do not pay at inquiry stage. Once the proposed story is approved, we can provide a secure PayPal payment request or hosted checkout link, subject to account eligibility. We confirm the final price and terms before you pay. If we cannot publish after collecting payment, contact us for a refund; other issues are reviewed case by case.
