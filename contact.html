Contact NewsTrendus

See an incorrect source credit, outdated link, misleading headline categorization or a technical problem? We welcome specific corrections and constructive feedback. NewsTrendus is a source-aggregating project, not the original publisher of the reports shown in our brief pages.

Public feedback & corrections channel

For an issue involving this website, please submit a report through our public GitHub repository's issue tracker. Include the NewsTrendus page address, the original publisher's URL where relevant, and a short explanation of what needs changing. This option is free, but you need a GitHub account and your message will be publicly visible.

Submit a public website issue ↗
Privacy: Do not submit passwords, phone numbers, financial information or other personal data in GitHub issues. For private concerns or a rights-holder request, do not post personal or confidential documents publicly. The project does not currently provide a private support mailbox.

Corrections to third-party reports

If you believe a claim in the original published article is inaccurate, please contact the original news publisher directly for a correction. We can amend or remove the brief on NewsTrendus, but we cannot edit a third-party publication.

Copyright concerns

If you own rights to text displayed in one of our RSS previews and wish to request removal, identify the NewsTrendus page, original URL and the specific content involved through the public issue tracker without sharing confidential documentation. We can then review the site content. Rights-holders requiring confidential correspondence should be aware that this public tracker is not a private communication channel.

Advertising

For information about how advertising technology may handle your data, see our privacy policy. We do not accept paid editorial placement through this channel.
