About our publication

News for curious Americans, with context and clear sources.

NewsTrendus is an independent US-focused online news discovery and explanation project. We organize fresh headlines into easy-to-browse sections such as US News, World, Technology, Gaming, Science, Sports, Business and Entertainment. The edition is designed for US readers; our publication does not claim to be physically headquartered in the United States.

What makes this site different?

Our homepage brings together two useful signals: recent headlines provided by news publishers in their RSS feeds, and trending search topics from Google Trends for the United States. They serve different purposes. A publisher's report can offer evidence about an event, while a trending search shows what people are looking up. We keep those concepts separate.

For readers who want to go deeper, our explainers cover topics like confirming GTA VI announcements, interpreting search trends and checking rapidly developing reports. Unlike our automatically collected headlines, these guides are created as original educational resources and updated when reviewed.

What are news briefs?

When you open a headline on NewsTrendus, you can read a short source-attributed preview without leaving our website. Most text is a brief from publisher-provided RSS metadata. Where source permissions and completeness checks allow it, NASA agency-authored releases and selected KFF Health News articles are displayed in full with attribution. These remain the original publishers' work, not reporting by NewsTrendus. The source link remains available for quotations, reporting details and the complete context.

Our approach to trust

We do not treat popularity as proof. Our graphics are illustrative, not photos of the specific incident, unless expressly identified otherwise. Our headlines are refreshed automatically, so an individual item may change after the original publisher updates its feed. For how we handle corrections, read our editorial standards.

Advertising and independence

NewsTrendus uses Google AdSense technology where approved. Advertising supports the cost of running the publication but does not determine which sources appear in the news feed, and advertisers do not write our editorial guides. We never ask readers to click ads to support us.

Who is responsible for the site?

This is an independently operated web project. Our public contact and feedback page explains how to report a technical problem, incorrect attribution or a correction request. We do not claim to be affiliated with major news networks, Google, Rockstar Games or the US government.
