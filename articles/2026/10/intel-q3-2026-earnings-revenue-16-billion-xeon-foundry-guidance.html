Intel Q3 2026 Earnings: Revenue Hits $16.5B, Xeon Demand Rises, Q4 Outlook Softens

Intel's server business accelerated, while its fourth-quarter forecast points to a tougher PC environment. The GAAP and adjusted numbers tell different stories.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 29, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not a photo of the reported event or the exact person, product or research. Photo: Sergei Starostin / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Intel posted $16.5 billion in Q3 2026 revenue, 21% growth and $0.60 GAAP EPS. The company sees Q4 sales of $15B–$16B amid PC weakness.

Intel's Q3 2026 earnings showed stronger data-center demand and higher overall revenue, but the company warned that the PC market could remain soft into next year. In its October 29 release, Intel reported $16.5 billion in third-quarter revenue, a 21% increase from $13.7 billion a year earlier.

How did Intel perform in the third quarter?

Intel reported $0.60 in GAAP diluted earnings per share attributable to Intel, down from $0.90 in the year-earlier quarter. On a non-GAAP basis, diluted EPS rose to $0.52 from $0.23. The difference reflects accounting adjustments, so neither number should be substituted for the other without explaining the basis.

GAAP gross margin rose to 43.0%, compared with 38.2% a year earlier. Intel said operating cash flow reached $5.1 billion during the quarter. Its earnings announcement characterized the period as an improvement in execution and supply expansion, while acknowledging ongoing industry constraints.

Why are Intel Xeon and data-center sales in focus?

The Data Center and AI segment reported $7.3 billion in revenue, up 78% year over year. Intel described demand for Xeon processors as particularly strong and said increasing supply was a near-term priority. The Client Computing and Physical AI group, which includes much of the PC-facing business, generated $8.1 billion, down 5%.

This split helps explain why the market is watching INTC stock through two different lenses. Server processors benefit from AI-related computing demand and enterprise upgrades, while PC demand remains vulnerable to replacement-cycle timing and pricing pressure.

What does the Intel Foundry revenue number mean?

Intel reported $6.7 billion in foundry segment revenue, up 58%. However, Intel's segment presentation includes intersegment transactions, and the company separately listed $6.3 billion of eliminations. Readers should not simply add the foundry, PC and data-center figures to calculate total consolidated sales.

Foundry progress matters for Intel's longer-term manufacturing strategy, but revenue growth alone does not prove that the segment has achieved durable profitability. External customer adoption, manufacturing yields, capital requirements and margins remain separate questions.

What is Intel's Q4 2026 guidance?

Intel forecast $15.0 billion to $16.0 billion in fourth-quarter revenue, a range below Q3 sales. Its midpoint outlook called for GAAP EPS of $0.10 and non-GAAP EPS of $0.25, with corresponding gross margins of about 37.1% and 38.0%.

Management said it expects PC industry softness to continue at least through the first half of 2027, even as demand for its server products remains robust. Forecasts can change with customer orders, supply availability and manufacturing execution.

What should investors monitor next?

Watch whether Intel can turn strong Xeon demand into shipments, maintain gross-margin improvements and demonstrate progress in its manufacturing roadmap. For investors, the key comparison is not just the 21% revenue increase, but how that growth translates into sustainable profits and cash generation across business lines.
