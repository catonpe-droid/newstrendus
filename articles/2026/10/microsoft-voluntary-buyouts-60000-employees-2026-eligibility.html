Microsoft Buyouts 2026: About 60,000 Employees Eligible, Not Laid Off

Microsoft's new voluntary exit offer could reach roughly one-quarter of its workforce, but eligibility is not a layoff notice and the number of employees who will leave remains unknown.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 26, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not the reported event, people or companies. Photo: Jakub Zerdzicki / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Reuters reported October 26 that approximately 60,000 Microsoft employees can consider voluntary buyouts across four countries. The company has not announced 60,000 layoffs.

What Microsoft announced on October 26

UNITED STATES, Oct. 26, 2026 — Microsoft is making a time-limited voluntary exit offer available to about 60,000 employees, according to Reuters reporting based on company comments and an internal memo. The offer allows qualifying staff to decide whether to leave with a package. It does not mean that 60,000 people have been fired, accepted the offer or will depart.

Microsoft Chief People Officer Amy Coleman described the program as another option for employees considering a future outside the company and a way for participating organizations to adapt to changing needs. The number of actual departures, total severance costs and full team-level eligibility were not disclosed in the public reporting available Monday.

Which Microsoft employees are eligible for the buyout?

Microsoft told Reuters that the offer is available to eligible employees at level 70 and below in participating teams in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and India. Reported participating functions include AI experiences, Copilot, finance, human resources, Xbox and Windows + Devices. Marketing, commercial business and security were among the excluded groups identified by a source familiar with the matter.

That is not a complete company-wide eligibility roster. A worker should rely on their personalized HR communication rather than assuming that a colleague's job title, office location or product group guarantees the same offer.

Is this a layoff, and when must workers decide?

A voluntary buyout is an invitation to separate from an employer on stated terms; a layoff is an employer-directed job elimination. The two can have different consequences for benefits, stock awards and unemployment eligibility. Axios Seattle reported that eligible employees have until early December to decide, with departures expected around year-end. Exact deadlines and packages should be checked against the offer document.

The public reports describe severance and transition support, but do not establish a universal dollar amount. Employees should review insurance continuation, unused leave, equity vesting, rehire provisions and any release of claims before signing.

Why is Microsoft offering exits amid AI spending?

Microsoft has been expanding AI computing infrastructure while trying to manage costs. Reuters reported that the company forecast roughly $175 billion in calendar-year 2026 capital expenditure and counted 223,000 full-time employees at June 30. The buyout pool is therefore large, but it is not a prediction of headcount reduction.

The new program follows other restructuring actions, including a voluntary retirement offer earlier in 2026 and reported job cuts in July. Those events help explain the business backdrop without proving that any specific individual role is redundant or that AI directly replaced an employee.

What to watch next

Three figures remain unanswered: how many employees accept, what the program costs and how participating teams will operate afterward. The company's next financial disclosures may clarify those points. Employees should use official HR channels for personal terms; investors should distinguish reported eligibility from confirmed departures.
