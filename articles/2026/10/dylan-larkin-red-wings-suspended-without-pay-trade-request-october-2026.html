Why Did the Red Wings Suspend Dylan Larkin? Trade Request and What Happens Next

The Detroit Red Wings suspended Dylan Larkin without pay on October 29 after the club said he would no longer report to play. Detroit says it will continue exploring trades, but no deal has been announced.

At a glance: The Detroit Red Wings suspended Dylan Larkin without pay on October 29 after the club said he would no longer report to play. Detroit says it will continue exploring trades, but no deal has been announced.

Detroit suspended former captain Dylan Larkin without pay on October 29, 2026, after the Red Wings said he would no longer play for the organization while seeking a trade. The team has not announced a completed trade, and the player's approval remains a key condition for any move.

Why was Dylan Larkin suspended?

Interim general manager Shawn Horcoff said the Red Wings were informed that Larkin did not intend to continue playing for Detroit. The club responded by placing him on an unpaid suspension and said it would continue to explore trade opportunities. The NHL's October 30 report includes the club's statement and Horcoff's explanation.

That makes the current status more specific than a routine healthy scratch or a short-term injury absence. It also does not mean Larkin has already been traded, released or signed with another team. Associated Press reporting independently confirmed the suspension and the team's account of the dispute.

When did Larkin request a trade?

Larkin's request dates to the 2026 offseason. The former captain had been seeking a change for months before the October 29 decision. Detroit removed his captaincy on September 11 and began the new season without a player wearing the 'C.' The league's September account documents that earlier development.

At the time of the suspension, Larkin was 30 and had recorded two goals and four assists in 10 games this season, according to the NHL. He had spent his entire NHL career with Detroit after being selected in the first round of the 2014 draft. The length of that association helps explain why the dispute has drawn attention beyond the team's standings.

Can the Red Wings trade Larkin to any team?

Not automatically. Larkin has trade protection in his contract, so Detroit must obtain his approval for a destination covered by that protection. Horcoff said a deal must also make sense for the franchise's long-term interests. The team can keep negotiating, but an interested club and a workable return are not the same as an executed transaction.

Larkin signed an eight-year contract in March 2023 carrying an $8.7 million average annual value, the NHL reported. The financial commitment and approval requirement are material considerations for potential partners. Publicly circulated destination lists should not be treated as evidence that a particular team has reached an agreement.

Did Larkin play against the Blackhawks?

No. Coach Todd McLellan said before Thursday's game that Larkin would not be in the lineup. Detroit went on to beat Chicago 4–1, according to the NHL game-day report. The win does not resolve the roster situation or establish when negotiations will conclude.

What happens next for Larkin and Detroit?

The next verified milestone would be an official trade, a change to the suspension, or another statement from the club or player representatives. As of this report, none of those outcomes has been confirmed. Fans looking for a concrete update should use the NHL newsroom and team announcements rather than treating proposed destinations as completed moves.
