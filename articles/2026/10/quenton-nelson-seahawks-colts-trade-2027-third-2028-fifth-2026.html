Quenton Nelson Traded to Seahawks: Colts Receive 2027 Third- and 2028 Fifth-Round Picks

Seattle's official announcement says the All-Pro guard is joining from Indianapolis pending a physical, in exchange for two future draft selections.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 27, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Gezer Amorim / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Seattle announced its October 27, 2026 trade for eight-time Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson, pending physical. See draft-pick terms and what the move changes.

What are the confirmed Quenton Nelson trade terms?

SEATTLE, Oct. 27, 2026 — The Seattle Seahawks announced they have acquired All-Pro offensive guard Quenton Nelson from the Indianapolis Colts in a trade pending a physical. Seattle's official team report says Indianapolis will receive a 2027 third-round draft pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

The pending-physical qualification matters. It means the clubs announced the deal, but the public statement did not present the medical process as complete at the time of publication. Readers should distinguish the agreed terms from any subsequent roster or transaction update.

Why did Seattle target the veteran guard?

Nelson has been among the NFL's most decorated offensive linemen since Indianapolis selected him sixth overall in the 2018 draft. Seattle's report credits him with eight Pro Bowl selections and three first-team All-Pro honors, with additional second-team recognition.

Guards work on the interior offensive line, helping protect the quarterback and establish running lanes. Adding a proven veteran could address a high-impact position without using a current draft pick, but how much he improves the unit will depend on health, system fit and actual game performance.

How has Nelson performed in 2026?

According to statistics cited by the Seahawks, Nelson had allowed no sacks across 275 pass-blocking snaps and a 2.2% pressure rate at the time of the announcement. These are team-reported figures attributed to Pro Football Reference and should not be mistaken for a prediction of future results.

Seattle also highlighted his durability, noting he missed only four games in 2021 across a lengthy starting career. Availability is relevant to a midseason acquisition, but no past durability record can guarantee that a player will avoid injury going forward.

What do the Colts receive and why does timing matter?

The Colts gain future draft capital rather than an immediate replacement player under the announced terms. The return is a third-round selection in 2027 and a fifth-rounder in 2028, not two picks in the same draft.

The Associated Press described the move against the backdrop of Indianapolis' difficult 2026 start and a change in football leadership. Those broader circumstances help explain why a veteran might be moved, but the teams' public statements do not establish every internal motivation behind the trade.

When could Nelson play for the Seahawks?

Seattle subsequently reported waiving guard Christian Haynes to make room on its 53-man roster. That is a separate roster move and does not by itself establish Nelson's first game appearance. The club will still determine his availability and integration into the offensive line.

For fans following the NFL trade deadline, the most useful checkpoints are the official transaction listing, the team's injury report and the next game-day active roster. A trade announcement alone is not confirmation of snaps played or a starting assignment.
