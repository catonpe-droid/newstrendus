Christian McCaffrey Injury Update: 49ers RB Questionable vs Falcons

The San Francisco running back exited during the first half after a productive opening drive; no confirmed recovery timetable was available.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not an image of the reported event, product or people. Photo: Pixabay / Pexels (free-use license).

Quick answer: Christian McCaffrey left the 49ers–Falcons game October 25, 2026, with a reported thigh injury and was listed questionable to return. What is confirmed so far.

ATLANTA — San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey left the team’s October 25, 2026 game against the Atlanta Falcons in the first half with a reported thigh injury. He was listed as questionable to return in reports available during the second quarter. A definitive diagnosis or return timetable had not been established at that point.

What happened to Christian McCaffrey?

McCaffrey received attention from medical staff after an early-game sequence and spent time in the medical tent before heading toward the locker room. Reuters described the issue as a thigh injury. Some live commentary initially referred to a knee concern, illustrating why a preliminary in-game label should not be treated as a final diagnosis.

The running back had four carries for 29 yards and a touchdown, along with three receptions for 45 yards, before leaving. Those numbers reflect the first-half reports, not his eventual full-game statistics.

McCaffrey's official game status at the time

The in-game designation was questionable to return, according to Reuters and contemporaneous local reporting. That means his availability was uncertain; it does not establish that he had been ruled out for the rest of the contest.

No verified scan result, recovery period or next-game availability was included in those initial reports. Readers should avoid treating online speculation about a long-term injury as confirmed medical information.

Who replaced him for San Francisco?

Rookie running back Kaelon Black entered in McCaffrey’s absence and scored on a 30-yard run during the second quarter. The play helped the 49ers maintain their early lead, although it did not answer the larger question of how San Francisco would distribute rushing and receiving touches if McCaffrey remained out.

The 49ers were already dealing with other injury-related absences entering Week 7. NBC Sports Bay Area reported before kickoff that receiver Mike Evans and defensive lineman Nick Bosa were ruled out, as was receiver Deebo Samuel.

Why this matters for the unbeaten 49ers

San Francisco entered the matchup at 6–0. McCaffrey’s versatility is important because he contributes as both a runner and pass catcher, especially when the receiver group is depleted.

A short-term absence during one game and a longer injury absence are different situations. The team’s Week 8 bye offers a scheduled break after the Falcons matchup, but it would be premature to say that any specific injury will heal during that interval.

Latest confirmed information and next update

As of the first-half reports on October 25, McCaffrey was questionable to return with a reported thigh issue. This article intentionally does not state a final Falcons–49ers score or a later injury ruling that had not been verified when it was prepared.
