McDonald's Holiday Drinks 2026: Five Cookie Butter and Cranberry Options Arrive Nov. 3

The chain confirmed five limited-time seasonal beverages for participating U.S. restaurants, including three Cookie Butter coffees and two cranberry drinks.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 27, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Nguyen Huy / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: McDonald's 2026 holiday drinks start November 3: Cookie Butter Frappé, Latte, Iced Coffee and two Frosted Cranberry drinks. Menu details and availability.

When do McDonald's holiday drinks launch in the U.S.?

CHICAGO, Oct. 27, 2026 — McDonald's announced a new lineup of five limited-time holiday beverages arriving November 3 at participating restaurants across the United States. The company published the list in its official U.S. Menu Spotter update dated October 27, giving customers a source to check before the launch.

The announced date is a launch date, not proof that every restaurant will carry every drink at all times. Local availability can vary, and McDonald's did not publish a universal price for the lineup in the company announcement reviewed here. Customers should check their local menu or app rather than assume a nationwide fixed price.

What are the three new Cookie Butter McCafé drinks?

The coffee-focused part of the lineup consists of a Cookie Butter Frappé, a Cookie Butter Latte and a Cookie Butter Iced Coffee. McDonald's describes the flavor profile as cookie butter with caramel cookie crumbs and sweet vanilla whipped topping.

These are three distinct drink formats, not three different flavor announcements. The Frappé is the blended option, the Latte is a coffee-and-milk style drink, and the Iced Coffee is served cold. A consumer looking for the least dessertlike choice should still review the actual ingredient and nutrition information once posted; the word coffee alone does not establish sugar or calorie content.

What is the Frosted Cranberry Splash?

The Frosted Cranberry Splash with Sprite combines Sprite with orange and cranberry flavors, then adds cold foam and cranberry sprinkles. It is presented as a festive soft-drink alternative rather than an espresso-based McCafé coffee.

McDonald's also announced a Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizer using the seasonal cranberry profile. The Energizer is offered with regular Red Bull or Red Bull Zero. The company describes the drink as an energy-beverage option, so customers who monitor caffeine intake should review the serving size and ingredients before ordering.

Will the drinks be available at every McDonald's?

The company says the five drinks are coming to participating restaurants nationwide for a limited time. That language matters: a nationwide promotion does not guarantee identical availability in every store, and franchise locations can have differing inventory or equipment.

Before making a trip, check the selected restaurant in the McDonald's app. A location's menu, rather than a social-media photo, is the better guide to actual offerings and prices. Promotional photographs can also differ from a drink prepared at the counter.

How does this compare with the chain's other seasonal promotions?

The holiday beverage launch follows the chain's October 20 Disney Villains Boo Buckets promotion and an earlier expansion of McCafé drink categories. McDonald's has been testing broader beverage formats, including Refreshers and Energizers, as it tries to capture more occasions beyond a traditional morning coffee order.

Independent first-taste coverage from PEOPLE and Allrecipes describes the cookie-butter drinks as sweet and dessert-oriented. Those impressions are subjective reviews, not company nutrition claims. The confirmed consumer takeaway is the November 3 start date, five named options and limited-time participation conditions.
