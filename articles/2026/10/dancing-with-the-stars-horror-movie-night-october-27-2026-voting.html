DWTS Horror Movie Night 2026 Results: Tyler Cameron, Jackson Olson Eliminated

Ten couples enter Horror Movie Night, with two eliminations expected after last week's no-elimination episode. Results are not yet confirmed.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 28, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Prime Cinematics / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: DWTS Horror Movie Night Oct. 27 results: Tyler Cameron and Jackson Olson were eliminated; Harry Shum Jr. earned a perfect 40/40. Scores and voting explained.

Who was eliminated on Dancing With the Stars October 27?

Tyler Cameron and professional partner Sharna Burgess, along with Jackson Olson and professional partner Emma Slater, were eliminated from Dancing With the Stars on Tuesday, October 27, 2026. The double elimination came at the end of Season 35's Horror Movie Night. Page Six's post-show report identifies both departing couples, and TV Insider's episode recap independently confirms the elimination format.

The results reduced the remaining competition from ten pairs to eight. This was not a surprise third elimination or a technical disqualification: the show combined judges' scores and viewer votes from the previous Dedication Night and the Halloween-themed October 27 episode, after no couple went home the week before. As always, the televised results, rather than an unofficial online poll, determine who stays.

What were Tyler Cameron and Jackson Olson's scores?

Cameron and Burgess received 26 out of 40 from the four judges for their Halloween routine, bringing their reported two-week judges' total to 58 out of 80. Olson and Slater scored 35 out of 40 on October 27, with a two-week judges' total of 67 out of 80. Their different scores illustrate why a judges-only leaderboard cannot by itself predict the combined elimination outcome.

Viewer votes are an important part of the competition's format. The publicly reported totals above are judges' scores, not disclosed raw vote counts. Claims assigning precise audience-vote numbers to either eliminated couple would go beyond the confirmed information reviewed for this report.

Who received the first perfect score of Season 35?

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson earned a 40 out of 40 for their Saw-inspired contemporary routine. It was reported as the season's first perfect judges' score, giving the pair a two-night total of 76 out of 80. The performance stood out on a night when guest judge Shirley Ballas joined Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli.

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy, and Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, were reported at 74 out of 80 across the two nights. Those figures are useful for comparing the judges' assessment, but they are not a complete ranking of audience support. A perfect score guarantees praise from the panel, not an automatic place in the finale.

What was Horror Movie Night's format?

Disney's official October 22 press release scheduled the episode for October 27 on ABC and Disney+, with next-day availability on Hulu. The routines drew on films including Saw, The Omen, Sinners, Black Swan and Annabelle: Creation. The show used a four-judge panel and the two-week carryover to resolve the double elimination.

For viewers who missed the broadcast, the official ABC show page is the safest starting point for episode and replay availability. Local television and streaming access can vary, and unofficial recap clips do not replace the show's published result.

What happens next for DWTS Season 35?

Eight pairs remain in contention for the Mirrorball Trophy following the October 27 results. Their next dances, guest judges and any further format changes should be checked against the show's official schedule rather than inferred from speculation. This report was updated after Horror Movie Night with the confirmed eliminated couples and the reported top scores.
