DWTS Horror Movie Night 2026: Start Time, Voting and Double Elimination

Ten couples enter Horror Movie Night, with two eliminations expected after last week's no-elimination episode. Results are not yet confirmed.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 27, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

At a glance: Dancing With the Stars Season 35 returns Oct. 27 at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+. How voting works and why Horror Movie Night has two exits.

When is Dancing With the Stars on tonight?

Dancing With the Stars Season 35 is scheduled to return Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 8 p.m. Eastern / 7 p.m. Central on ABC, with streaming on Disney+. For viewers on the West Coast, that corresponds to 5 p.m. Pacific for the live Eastern feed, though local broadcast and streaming access can differ by provider. ABC's official show page lists the Tuesday 8/7c slot.

The October 27 theme is Horror Movie Night, a Halloween-week program featuring routines inspired by frightening films. This is a preview written before the live episode begins in the United States, not a post-show recap. No October 27 elimination outcome is asserted here.

Why are two couples expected to leave?

Ten celebrity-professional partnerships remained after the previous Dedication Night episode ended without an elimination. Contemporary previews from TV Insider and Parade say two couples are scheduled to be eliminated during Horror Movie Night, with last week's votes and judges' scores carried forward.

That setup changes the stakes of a routine evening: a single strong dance may not fully overcome the accumulated score and audience-vote picture. The show's exact outcome depends on the live results, so any social post claiming to know the eliminated pairs in advance should be treated skeptically.

How does DWTS voting work?

Viewer participation is tied to the live broadcast window. The show's elimination process combines judges' scores with audience votes rather than relying on dance scores alone. Fans should consult ABC's official Dancing With the Stars page and the show's verified voting instructions for current eligibility, voting methods and deadlines; unofficial online polls do not count toward the television competition.

Viewers outside the Eastern time zone should pay attention to the live voting period rather than assuming that a delayed local broadcast provides a new voting window. Availability on ABC, Disney+ and next-day streaming services also varies by subscription and region.

Who is judging Horror Movie Night?

The regular panel includes Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli. TV Insider reports that Shirley Ballas joins as a guest judge for the October 27 installment. That means the scoring scale and comparisons with previous episodes may require care when evaluating totals.

The remaining field has been narrowed from the season's original lineup, but a pre-show list should not be confused with a verified final ranking. Scores, any announced eliminations and next week's pairings should be confirmed after ABC broadcasts the results.

What should viewers check after the show?

Look for the names of both eliminated couples, the judges' scores and any official changes to the remaining lineup. Until those are released, the reliable takeaway is the scheduled start time, Horror Movie Night theme and anticipated double elimination.
