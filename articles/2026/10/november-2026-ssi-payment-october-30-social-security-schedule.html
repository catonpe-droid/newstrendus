November 2026 SSI Payment Arrives October 30: Full Social Security Dates

The Social Security Administration's 2026 calendar moves November's SSI deposit to Friday, October 30; it is an early payment, not extra benefits.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Quick answer: SSI for November 2026 is scheduled for Friday, October 30. Regular Social Security dates in November vary by eligibility and birth date.

UNITED STATES, Oct. 25, 2026 — People who receive Supplemental Security Income (SSI) should expect their November 2026 payment on Friday, October 30, according to the Social Security Administration's official payment calendar. The first day of November falls on a Sunday, so the agency advances the payment to the preceding business day.

Why is November SSI paid in October?

SSI is generally scheduled for the first day of each month. When that date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, the payment is made earlier. In 2026, November 1 is a Sunday, so the scheduled deposit moves to Friday, October 30. That means recipients can see two SSI deposits in October: the normal October 1 payment for October and the October 30 payment for November.

These are not two payments for the same benefit month. The October 30 deposit is November's regular SSI benefit, issued ahead of the calendar month. People budgeting for rent, food or utilities should treat it as November income rather than a bonus.

November 2026 Social Security payment dates

SSI and Social Security retirement, survivor or disability benefits have different payment schedules. For most Social Security beneficiaries, the date depends on their birth date. SSA's published 2026 calendar shows these November dates:

  • October 30: November SSI benefit.
  • November 3: Social Security for people receiving benefits before May 1997 and certain people receiving both SSI and Social Security.
  • November 10: Second-Wednesday group, birth dates 1–10; moved forward because Veterans Day falls on November 11.
  • November 18: Third-Wednesday group, birth dates 11–20.
  • November 25: Fourth-Wednesday group, birth dates 21–31.

The published dates describe when SSA schedules payments, not a guarantee of the exact hour an individual bank posts a deposit. Some people have different arrangements, so their own SSA account and benefit notice remain the best references.

Will SSI recipients get another deposit in November?

There is no separately scheduled SSI payment dated in November because that month's benefit is paid October 30. The next regularly scheduled SSI payment after that is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, for December. This calendar pattern sometimes fuels online claims that benefits have been canceled or that the government is sending an extra check; neither conclusion follows from the schedule.

What to do if your deposit is missing

First check your financial institution's deposit history and your official SSA payment schedule. The agency's printed calendar advises allowing three additional mailing days before contacting Social Security about an expected payment that has not arrived. Do not share account numbers or Social Security numbers in comments or with unsolicited callers claiming to fix a delayed payment.

November SSI questions

Is October 30, 2026 an extra SSI check?

No. It is the regular November SSI payment issued early.

Will there be an SSI payment on November 1?

No. November 1 is Sunday, so SSI is scheduled for Friday, October 30.

Where is the official payment calendar?
