Zelda Switch 2 40th Anniversary Edition Launches Oct. 29: U.S. Availability and Price Facts

Nintendo confirmed a Japan-only Japanese-language model and a separate Ocarina of Time release; an American price should not be inferred from yen.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 29, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not a photo of the reported event or the exact product or person. Photo: Michael Adeleye / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Nintendo's Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 arrives in Japan October 29 for ¥62,980. Here's what U.S. shoppers should know about region, games and accessories.

Nintendo's Switch 2 – The Legend of Zelda – 40th Anniversary Edition has an official October 29, 2026 release date in Japan. The specially decorated hardware marks four decades of the game franchise, but U.S. shoppers should be careful about interpreting launch-day headlines: Nintendo's detailed corporate release describes a Japanese-language, Japan-only model with a Japanese suggested retail price, not a confirmed nationwide U.S. price.

What is included with the Zelda anniversary Switch 2?

The console features Triforce motifs on the system's back, the dock and the Joy-Con 2 controllers. Nintendo lists the console, a pair of Joy-Con 2 controllers, straps, dock, grip, AC adapter, USB-C charging cable and HDMI cable in the Japan package. The company explicitly says no game is included.

This distinction matters because shoppers may mistake the Zelda branding for a bundle with a new Zelda title. It is a commemorative hardware design, and game purchases are separate.

How much does it cost, and is there a U.S. price?

Nintendo's September 8 corporate announcement sets a Japan MSRP of ¥62,980 including tax. That is the listed price for the Japan-only Japanese-language system. It is not an official dollar price, and a currency conversion would not account for different retail markets, taxes, import costs or regional hardware specifications.

Independent gaming coverage has discussed international availability and potential retailer listings, but shoppers should verify any American listing directly with Nintendo or an authorized seller before treating it as confirmed. The cited Japanese announcement alone does not establish a U.S. launch price or nationwide stock.

Are matching Zelda controllers and accessories available?

Nintendo announced a themed Pro Controller with display stand at ¥12,980 and a themed carrying case with screen protector at ¥3,980, both Japan prices including tax. These accessories have their own product listings and are not automatically included in the console package. Check the exact regional product description before buying.

When is the new Ocarina of Time game coming?

In a separate Nintendo announcement, the company said The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 is scheduled for November 5, 2026. Nintendo described revised visuals, voiced cutscenes, expanded dialogue and reworked controls. That software date is one week after the anniversary hardware's Japan launch; it does not mean the game ships in the console box.

What should U.S. buyers verify before importing?

Confirm the language and region of the actual hardware, supported online services, warranty coverage, power accessories, shipping charges and return policy. A Japan-only model may not offer the experience a U.S. customer expects from locally sold multilingual hardware. Do not rely on an unverified social post or a reseller's price as an official U.S. announcement.

For fans who simply want to play the new Zelda title, checking official U.S. game listings and supported hardware is safer than buying a commemorative import on the assumption that it includes software.
