Real Madrid Beat Barcelona 2–1: Mbappé's Late Goal Decides El Clásico

Kylian Mbappé scored twice, including the late winner, as Real Madrid beat Barcelona 2–1 at Camp Nou on October 25.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Quick answer: Barcelona 1, Real Madrid 2. Mbappé scored both Madrid goals, while Raphinha equalized for Barcelona before the late winner.

BARCELONA, Oct. 25, 2026 — Real Madrid defeated Barcelona 2–1 in a closely contested El Clásico at Camp Nou, with Kylian Mbappé scoring both Madrid goals. Barcelona's Raphinha had equalized in the second half, but Madrid struck late to take three points and narrow the gap in the Spanish league title race.

How did Real Madrid beat Barcelona?

Madrid's opening goal came in the 41st minute when a low attacking move ended with Gerard Martín's attempted clearance deflecting off Mbappé and into the net. Barcelona had plenty of possession and chances, but the visitors' pressing and counterattacks repeatedly threatened to turn turnovers into clear openings.

After halftime, Raphinha equalized around the hour mark following a clever passing move involving Pedri. That gave Barcelona renewed momentum. Goalkeeper Andriy Lunin also made important saves to keep Madrid in contention as the hosts pushed for a winner.

Mbappé's decisive late winner

With the score still 1–1 late in the second half, Jude Bellingham helped start another Madrid attack. Eduardo Camavinga carried the move into Barcelona's penalty area and squared the ball for Mbappé to finish. The winner arrived in the final minutes of regulation, leaving Barcelona too little time to respond.

The two-goal performance was the defining individual contribution, but the broader tactical story was Madrid's ability to withstand periods of Barcelona pressure and create high-value chances in transition. The result was confirmed in contemporary reports from The Guardian and UOL on October 25.

What happened to the La Liga title race?

Barcelona entered the match in front in the standings. Following the 2–1 result, contemporary coverage reported Barcelona on 27 points and Real Madrid on 24, with Atlético Madrid also in the race. League positions can change as other matchday fixtures are completed, so these figures are a snapshot of the immediate post-match situation.

For Barcelona, the defeat ended a long winning run and underlined the danger of leaving space behind an aggressive attack. For Madrid, the victory was a statement that the title contest remained open entering the next phase of the season.
