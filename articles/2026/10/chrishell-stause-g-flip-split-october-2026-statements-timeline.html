Chrishell Stause and G Flip Split: What Both Said and Their Relationship Timeline

The Selling Sunset alum and Australian musician publicly acknowledged their separation after more than four years together, while asking for care and privacy.

At a glance: Chrishell Stause and musician G Flip announced their separation on October 26, 2026, after more than four years together. Both shared statements expressing continuing care.

What Chrishell Stause and G Flip confirmed

UNITED STATES, Oct. 27, 2026 — Selling Sunset alum Chrishell Stause and Australian musician G Flip publicly confirmed their separation on Monday, October 26, after a relationship lasting more than four years. Stause addressed followers in an emotional video, and G Flip shared a separate written statement. Both indicated that they continued to care for one another and wanted privacy as they processed the change.

Extra reported excerpts of the public statements, while Rolling Stone Australia reported G Flip's confirmation. Those direct statements are stronger evidence of the split than unnamed-source speculation about private conversations.

Did either partner explain why the relationship ended?

Neither public statement offered a comprehensive, mutually confirmed explanation of the breakup. Stause said she did not have all the information or reasons and described the news as painful. G Flip said the experience was emotionally difficult and asked supporters to respect their privacy.

Some outlets reported claims from unnamed sources about who initiated the split. Those claims should not be confused with a shared explanation from the two people involved. It is possible to report a confirmed separation accurately without assigning blame, guessing at motives or treating private feelings as public facts.

Chrishell and G Flip's relationship timeline

The pair met in 2021 and made their relationship public in May 2022, during the period when Stause appeared in G Flip's music video for “Get Me Outta Here.” They held a Las Vegas wedding celebration in May 2023 and subsequently marked their relationship with additional ceremonies. Entertainment Weekly and The Independent reported the major milestones.

Coverage of their ceremonies has sometimes used the word “married,” but accounts of the legal documentation have varied. This report describes the publicly documented celebrations without claiming a legal status that has not been established in the source material reviewed.

Why the announcement drew attention in the United States

Stause became widely known to US audiences through the Netflix reality series Selling Sunset, while G Flip developed a following as a singer, songwriter and drummer. Their relationship crossed the entertainment and music worlds, making the announcement of interest to fans in both the United States and Australia.

The couple had also spoken publicly about hopes of building a family. Those earlier conversations form part of their public story, but they do not establish the reason for the separation. Reporting should avoid implying that fertility decisions or reality-TV pressures caused the breakup unless either party confirms that link.

What happens next?

As of October 27, neither person had announced a detailed plan for future personal arrangements in the statements reviewed. G Flip requested privacy, and Stause emphasized that she wanted the separation handled respectfully. Readers should be cautious about social-media posts that claim a reconciliation, legal filing or new relationship without direct confirmation.

The clearest verified update remains the October 26 statements themselves: the relationship has ended, both have expressed continued affection, and the reasons have not been fully explained publicly. Future professional projects should be assessed on their own announcements rather than through assumptions about the breakup.
