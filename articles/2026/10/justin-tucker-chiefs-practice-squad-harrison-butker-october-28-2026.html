Justin Tucker and the Chiefs: Practice-Squad Signing Report, Butker Status Explained

NFL Network says Kansas City is bringing in the former Ravens kicker after a difficult stretch for Harrison Butker. A practice-squad report is not an announcement that the Chiefs have replaced their starter.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 28, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Sean P. Twomey / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: NFL Network reports Kansas City is adding Justin Tucker to its practice squad. What the move means for Harrison Butker, game eligibility and the kicker competition.

KANSAS CITY, Mo., Oct. 28, 2026 — The Kansas City Chiefs are reportedly adding former Baltimore Ravens kicker Justin Tucker to their practice squad, a move that could create a new competition at one of football's most specialized positions. NFL.com, citing NFL Network and ESPN, reported the planned addition Wednesday. The Kansas City Star first reported the development, according to the league's account.

Did the Chiefs sign Justin Tucker?

The most precise description at the time of this report is that multiple outlets say the Chiefs are signing Tucker to their practice squad. NFL.com's report says Kansas City worked out several kickers before deciding on Tucker. The club's official 2026 transactions log had not yet displayed an October 28 entry in the version reviewed for this story. That does not disprove the reports; it means a transaction announcement and an independently updated roster should be checked before treating the move as formally completed.

Practice-squad membership differs from a spot on the 53-player active roster. A player may practice with the team and be considered for promotion or elevation under league rules, but the designation alone does not mean he will kick in the next game. Fans should watch the Chiefs' transaction wire and the weekly game-day roster, not just social-media claims about a starting change.

Why is Harrison Butker under pressure?

Butker remains the Chiefs' established kicker. However, NFL.com reported that he has missed five field-goal attempts and one extra point during the 2026 season. He also missed two field-goal tries in Kansas City's 20-3 loss to Seattle on Sunday. Those results explain why the organization might seek competition or additional depth, but they do not establish that coaches have already decided to bench him.

Special-teams decisions are affected by more than a kicker's season percentage. Field position, distance, weather, operation time, snap-and-hold execution and practice performance all matter. Tucker's reported workout performance is one piece of that evaluation, not a guarantee that his previous career accuracy will immediately return.

What is Tucker's NFL track record?

The 36-year-old built a long career in Baltimore, earning seven Pro Bowl selections and five first-team All-Pro honors, according to NFL.com's account. The league report lists his career field-goal accuracy at 89.1%, among the best marks in NFL history. It also notes that his 2024 season was less effective: he missed eight of 30 field-goal attempts and two extra-point tries.

He has not appeared in an NFL regular-season game since the 2024 season. That makes recent practice performance and roster readiness particularly relevant to any Chiefs decision. Past awards establish his résumé; they do not predict what he would produce in a new season.

Why was Tucker out of the league?

The Ravens released Tucker in 2025. The NFL subsequently suspended him for 10 games under its personal-conduct policy after allegations of inappropriate conduct during massage appointments. Tucker denied the allegations. The suspension is a league disciplinary action, not a criminal conviction, and the underlying accusations should not be described as adjudicated criminal facts. NFL.com's October 28 report provides the relevant background and the league's account of his eligibility.

That history is part of why the signing is attracting attention beyond ordinary special-teams roster news. Readers should distinguish the league's policy decision, the allegations and Tucker's response rather than combining them into an unsupported legal conclusion.

Will Tucker replace Butker this weekend?

There was no verified announcement at publication that Tucker would start or that Butker had been cut. A practice-squad move can give Kansas City options while keeping Butker on the active roster. If the Chiefs decide to elevate Tucker, that change should appear in a dated roster update before the game.

For fans and fantasy-football managers, the practical checkpoints are the Chiefs' official transactions, game-day inactives and any attributable comments from the coaching staff. Treat projected depth charts as forecasts until the club confirms its plan. This report will be updated if the roster status materially changes.
