Thunder Beat Clippers 108–101: Shai Scores 34 as Injuries Cloud OKC Win

Oklahoma City stayed unbeaten at 3–0, but injuries to Jalen Williams and two teammates complicated its victory over Los Angeles.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 26, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not the reported game or players. Photo: RODOLPHE ASENSI / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: The Thunder beat the Clippers 108–101 on October 25; Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 34, while multiple OKC players exited injured.

OKLAHOMA CITY, Oct. 25, 2026 — The Oklahoma City Thunder beat the Los Angeles Clippers 108–101 on Sunday, extending their perfect start to three games. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the scoring with 34 points, but the victory was overshadowed by injuries to several Oklahoma City players.

Thunder–Clippers final score and key performances

Gilgeous-Alexander finished with 34 points to lead Oklahoma City. Reserve guard Ajay Mitchell added 18 points, and center Isaiah Hartenstein recorded 12 points and 12 rebounds. The contributions mattered because the Clippers remained within striking distance deep into the fourth quarter.

Los Angeles pulled within one point at 97–96 on a Derrick Jones Jr. three-pointer with about three minutes remaining. The Thunder nevertheless protected their lead through the final possessions. The seven-point margin reflects a competitive finish rather than a wire-to-wire runaway.

Why Oklahoma City's bench was important

Mitchell supplied 18 points on 8-of-14 shooting, plus six rebounds, four assists and three steals in 27 minutes, according to the Associated Press report. His efficiency gave the Thunder a second reliable source of offense alongside Gilgeous-Alexander.

Hartenstein's 12 rebounds helped Oklahoma City control important possessions. When a game tightens in the fourth quarter, defensive rebounding and careful ball movement often matter as much as a leading scorer's total. Oklahoma City had enough contributions beyond its star guard to close out the game.

Jalen Williams injury: What is confirmed?

All-Star guard Jalen Williams went down during a drive late in the fourth quarter and left the floor on a stretcher, according to the Associated Press account. That report said towels covered his left leg as he was taken away. It did not provide a definitive medical diagnosis, so it would be premature to describe the extent of the injury or predict when he might return.

Oklahoma City also lost Cason Wallace, who had left-shoulder soreness, and Jaylin Williams, who had right-wrist soreness, earlier in the game. Neither returned. Readers seeking the latest medical information should look for subsequent official team updates rather than treat initial game observations as a diagnosis.

What the loss means for Los Angeles

The Clippers dropped to 1–2. Darius Garland led them with 16 points, while Kris Dunn and Brook Lopez scored 15 each. Their fourth-quarter push showed that Los Angeles could challenge Oklahoma City, but the Clippers did not get in front after cutting the margin to one point.

The Thunder improved to 3–0 and were scheduled to host Phoenix on Monday, October 26. The result strengthens their early record; the availability of the injured players may become the larger issue for the next game.
