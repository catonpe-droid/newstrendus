DeForest Buckner Traded to 49ers: Colts Get 2027 Fourth-Round Pick

The defensive tackle returns to the team that drafted him in 2016, while Indianapolis adds another future selection.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 28, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: SAULO LEITE / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: The Colts confirmed DeForest Buckner's October 27 trade to San Francisco for a 2027 fourth-round NFL draft pick. Career stats and roster impact.

What did the Colts and 49ers agree to?

The Indianapolis Colts announced on Tuesday, October 27, 2026 that they traded defensive tackle DeForest Buckner to the San Francisco 49ers in exchange for a 2027 fourth-round NFL Draft pick. The terms were confirmed in the Colts' own roster-moves announcement and separately reported by NFL.com and CBS Sports. It is a completed announced transaction, not a speculative trade proposal.

The move brings Buckner back to the franchise that chose him seventh overall in the 2016 draft. San Francisco previously sent him to Indianapolis in 2020 for a first-round selection. That history gives the return unusual context: both organizations know the player, but the draft compensation is substantially different six years later.

Why is Buckner returning to San Francisco?

Buckner, 32, gives the 49ers a proven interior defensive lineman with experience in their organization. The official Colts account says he had four sacks through the first seven games of the 2026 season after returning from neck surgery performed on New Year's Eve 2025. That is a meaningful football detail, but it should not be turned into a claim about his current medical condition beyond what the team has published.

The 49ers' immediate need and how they will deploy him are coaching questions that will become clearer with roster announcements and game participation. A trade itself does not establish his first game back, snap count or whether he will start. Those details should be checked against official team reports as they are released.

What did Buckner accomplish with Indianapolis?

Across 95 games and 90 starts for the Colts, the team's announcement credits Buckner with 47 sacks, 122 quarterback hits, 63 tackles for loss, six forced fumbles and 20 passes defended. He was a first-team Associated Press All-Pro in 2020 and earned Pro Bowl recognition in 2021 and 2023 while with Indianapolis.

Those numbers describe his Colts tenure, not a projection of what he will produce for San Francisco. For readers evaluating the deal, the distinction matters: the 49ers acquire a known veteran rather than an untested draft pick, while Indianapolis exchanges present-day defensive depth for future draft flexibility.

How does the deal fit the Colts' wider roster changes?

Indianapolis also announced a separate trade sending guard Quenton Nelson to Seattle on the same day. Together, the two moves added three future selections, including the fourth-rounder from San Francisco. The Colts' published 2027 list includes their own second-, third-, fourth-, fifth- and seventh-round choices, plus additional picks obtained from Seattle, San Francisco and Buffalo.

The timing signals a significant roster reshaping, but the team has not declared that every remaining veteran is available. It would be misleading to treat rumors about other players as completed deals. The confirmed facts are the Buckner-for-fourth transaction and the separately announced Nelson move.

What should fans watch next?

Follow the official 49ers roster report for Buckner's availability and the Colts' depth-chart changes on the defensive line. The exact 2027 fourth-round selection number cannot be known until the relevant draft order is established. This report was checked against team and league coverage available October 27, 2026.
