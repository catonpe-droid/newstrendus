Oklahoma State Beats Colorado 57–24 After 34–0 Second Half

Colorado led at halftime, but Oklahoma State scored 34 unanswered second-half points in a 57–24 Homecoming win.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not an image of the reported event or people. Photo: David Morris / Pexels (free-use license).

Quick answer: Oklahoma State won 57–24 after trailing 24–23 at halftime, outscoring Colorado 34–0 in the second half.

STILLWATER, Oklahoma — Oklahoma State beat Colorado 57–24 on Saturday, October 24, 2026, with an extraordinary second-half turnaround. Colorado led 24–23 at halftime, but the Cowboys scored 34 unanswered points after the break and kept the Buffaloes off the scoreboard for the final two quarters.

Oklahoma State vs Colorado: What changed after halftime?

The first half was competitive and high-scoring. Colorado quarterback Isaac Wilson threw for 222 yards and two touchdowns before the break and also ran for a score. Oklahoma State's Drew Mestemaker had already thrown for 264 yards and two touchdowns by halftime, but the Cowboys had settled for field goals on several drives.

That changed immediately in the third quarter. On Colorado's first offensive play after halftime, Wilson fumbled on a keeper. Safety Quinton Hammonds recovered at the Buffaloes' 24-yard line. Two plays later, Mestemaker connected with tight end Morgan McPhaul for a three-yard touchdown, and Oklahoma State took the lead for good.

How Oklahoma State turned one turnover into a rout

The Cowboys forced a punt on Colorado's next possession and then covered 78 yards for another touchdown. Caleb Hawkins contributed a 39-yard run and a 17-yard reception on that drive. What had been a one-point halftime deficit became a 37–24 Oklahoma State advantage, with Colorado's offense suddenly unable to match its earlier efficiency.

Colorado punted four times and committed three turnovers in the second half, according to Oklahoma State's official recap. The Cowboys held the Buffaloes to only 120 yards of offense after halftime. That combination of short fields, stops and productive drives explains why the final margin grew so sharply.

Drew Mestemaker and Sam Keltner stats

Mestemaker finished with 386 passing yards and four touchdowns. Hawkins rushed for 120 yards. Oklahoma State amassed 598 total yards and 28 first downs in the win, a performance that combined explosive passing with an effective running game.

Kicker Sam Keltner made five field goals, setting a school record for a single game. The five successful kicks also extended his consecutive field-goal streak to 18, tying the program mark cited by Oklahoma State. Those points mattered when the offense struggled to finish drives in the first half.

What the result means in the Big 12

Oklahoma State improved to 5–2 overall and 3–1 in Big 12 play. Colorado fell to 2–5 and 0–4 in the conference. The Cowboys have reasons to be encouraged by the second-half response, while Colorado will need to address the turnovers and stalled possessions that erased its halftime lead.

Oklahoma State's next listed game is at Iowa State, while Colorado is scheduled to host Kansas State. Both teams still have conference games ahead, so the standings will continue to change.
