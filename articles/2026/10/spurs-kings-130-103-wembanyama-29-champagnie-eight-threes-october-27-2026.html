Spurs Beat Kings 130-103: Wembanyama Scores 29, Champagnie Hits Eight 3s

San Antonio finally delivered a fast start at home, scoring 80 points before halftime against Sacramento.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 28, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: cottonbro studio / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: San Antonio beat Sacramento 130-103 on Oct. 27, 2026. Wembanyama had 29 points and 10 boards; Champagnie made eight 3s. Game recap and key numbers.

Spurs vs. Kings: final score and leading scorers

The San Antonio Spurs beat the Sacramento Kings 130-103 on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at Frost Bank Center. Victor Wembanyama delivered 29 points and 10 rebounds, while Julian Champagnie scored 26 and converted eight of his 13 three-point attempts. The result gave San Antonio its first home win of the new season and moved its record to 2-2.

Sacramento fell to 0-4 despite 24 points from De'Andre Hunter, 22 from Zach LaVine and a productive all-around night by Domantas Sabonis, who finished with 20 points, 14 rebounds and eight assists. The final margin was 27 points, but the shape of the game was established much earlier than the fourth quarter.

Why the first half decided the game

San Antonio scored 39 points in the first quarter and 41 in the second, taking an 80-point total into halftime. The Spurs had struggled to find that kind of rhythm in their first two home games. Against Sacramento, they attacked quickly and used perimeter shooting to keep the defense stretched.

Wembanyama set the tone with 15 points in the opening period. His work near the rim gave the Kings a difficult choice: send extra help into the paint or risk leaving shooters available outside. Champagnie made that trade-off expensive. The Spurs finished 17-for-37 from three-point range, according to the Associated Press game report.

Champagnie's shooting was more than a supporting detail

Eight made threes in a game can dominate a headline, but the strategic effect is just as important. Sacramento could not comfortably collapse on Wembanyama while Champagnie kept converting open perimeter looks. San Antonio's spacing made its offense less dependent on one player creating every possession.

Wembanyama made 14 of 21 field-goal attempts and repeatedly finished above the rim. His 29-point total came within a team performance in which all five Spurs starters reached double figures. Dylan Harper contributed 13 points, six rebounds and six assists, offering another way to move the ball rather than stopping possessions with isolated drives.

What Sacramento can take from the loss

Sabonis' 20-14-8 line shows the Kings still generated individual production. But efficient scoring by a few players could not offset San Antonio's early burst. A team allowing 80 first-half points has very little margin to manage missed shots or defensive breakdowns later.

The Kings will need to examine transition coverage, defensive communication around the arc and the way they handle an opponent who can score both inside and outside. Those are observable areas for review, not proof that one loss defines Sacramento's season. It is still the opening week.

What comes next for both teams?

San Antonio was scheduled to visit Utah on Wednesday, while Sacramento's next listed game was at New Orleans on Thursday. Readers following the teams should use the NBA's official game center for the box score and any later corrections. This recap covers the completed October 27 contest, not a prediction of either team's next result.
