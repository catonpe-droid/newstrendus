Knicks Beat Pistons 111-97: Towns' 27 Points, 20 Rebounds End Skid

A 37-13 third quarter flipped a slow start into New York's first win of the 2026-27 season.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 28, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Onur Bahadır / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Knicks beat Pistons 111-97 on Oct. 27, 2026. Karl-Anthony Towns had 27 points and 20 rebounds as New York dominated the third quarter.

Knicks vs. Pistons final score and top performers

The New York Knicks beat the Detroit Pistons 111-97 on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at Madison Square Garden. Karl-Anthony Towns led the way with 27 points and 20 rebounds, and Jalen Brunson added 23 points. Detroit entered without a loss, while New York was seeking its first victory of the season.

The result put the defending champion Knicks at 1-3 and the Pistons at 3-1. That contrast is central to the story: New York needed to reverse a poor opening stretch, and Detroit was trying to extend an unbeaten start. The result was not a wire-to-wire Knicks victory; New York had to recover from a difficult first quarter.

How a 37-13 third quarter changed the game

Detroit led 38-25 after one quarter. New York answered 26-12 in the second, cutting the deficit to a one-point halftime lead, then delivered a 37-13 third quarter to take command. The Knicks finished that period ahead 88-63. The 25-point cushion was large enough to withstand a more productive Detroit fourth quarter.

The swing is clearer when viewed by halves: Detroit scored only 25 combined points in the second and third quarters while New York scored 63. The Knicks' defensive improvement and more efficient possessions did more to decide the game than the final 14-point margin alone suggests. Brunson and Mikal Bridges helped ignite the second-half run.

Towns controlled the glass while Brunson recovered

Towns' 20 rebounds gave New York repeated opportunities to end Detroit possessions or extend its own. He also supplied reliable scoring around the basket. Brunson had struggled with his shot in the opening games and began Tuesday slowly, but he became more effective after halftime. Bridges scored 12 points and found his outside shooting rhythm.

New York's advantage was not flawless. The Knicks still committed 18 turnovers, according to a detailed SNY game breakdown. But they finished with 49 rebounds to Detroit's 45, shot better from three-point range and made more of their free throws. Those advantages helped neutralize the turnover problem.

What went wrong for Detroit?

Cade Cunningham recorded 19 points, nine rebounds and eight assists. Paul Reed scored 22 with nine rebounds while starting in place of injured center Jalen Duren. Detroit's opening quarter showed that its offense could create chances, but the middle two quarters became a long stretch of missed shots and fewer productive possessions.

The Pistons shot 9-for-37 from three-point range, or about 24%, in the published statistical recap. Poor perimeter conversion made it harder to respond as New York increased its defensive pressure. Detroit's strong fourth quarter narrowed the score but did not erase the earlier deficit.

What's next for New York and Detroit?

The Knicks were scheduled to visit the Chicago Bulls on Wednesday, October 28, while the Pistons were due to host the Charlotte Hornets. Game times, injury designations and lineup changes should be checked against the NBA's official schedule before tipoff. Tuesday's result is an early-season reset for New York, not proof that all of its opening-week problems have disappeared.
