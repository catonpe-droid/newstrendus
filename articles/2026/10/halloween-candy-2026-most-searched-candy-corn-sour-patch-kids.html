Halloween Candy 2026: Sour Patch Kids Lead Searches as Candy Corn Surges

Seasonal search data reveal a sharp Halloween jump for candy corn, but sales-based rankings tell a different story.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not an image of the reported event, product or people. Photo: Kristina Paukshtite / Pexels (free-use license).

Quick answer: New 2026 Google Trends-based research ranks Sour Patch Kids first for U.S. candy searches, while candy corn jumps to No. 2 near Halloween. Search is not sales.

UNITED STATES — With Halloween approaching on October 31, new research using Google Trends search data shows Sour Patch Kids leading U.S. candy interest over the past year. Candy corn rises sharply as the holiday approaches, ranking second during the three weeks before Halloween, according to Innerbody Research.

Which candy ranks first in 2026 searches?

Innerbody analyzed Google search interest for 34 major candy brands and varieties across the United States. Its full-year national top five were Sour Patch Kids, Skittles, Snickers, Starburst and Twix. KitKat, Reese’s, candy corn, Twizzlers and Nerds completed the reported top ten.

This is a ranking of relative online search attention, not purchases or household consumption. Someone searching for a candy recipe, brand or debate may never buy the product.

Why candy corn jumps before Halloween

Candy corn ranked eighth in the full-year search analysis but moved to second during the three weeks before Halloween, trailing only Sour Patch Kids. The result reflects the candy’s unusually seasonal appeal and the annual conversation about whether people love or dislike it.

Other sweets also gain attention near October 31. Innerbody reported M&M’s moving from nineteenth in full-year searches to eleventh in the Halloween window, while Milky Way and Haribo also improved their search positions.

Which states show the strongest candy interest?

The study placed Utah first among states for overall candy-related search interest, followed by Nebraska and Iowa. The researchers highlighted additional Midwestern states including Wisconsin, Missouri and Ohio among the top ten.

A state’s search intensity is not equivalent to pounds of candy purchased. Rankings can be affected by population-adjustment methods, search habits and the brands included in the study.

Why another ranking puts M&M's at No. 1

A separate 2026 ranking covered by NBC Dallas–Fort Worth placed M&M’s first in projected Halloween-season sales, with Sour Patch Kids second. That ranking draws on historical sales and industry information rather than the same Google Trends search methodology.

The apparent disagreement is useful: search popularity and sales popularity measure different things. A candy can generate a surge of curiosity without leading checkout totals. Headlines that combine the two into a single ‘favorite candy’ claim risk misleading readers.

What shoppers should take from the numbers

The rankings can help explain what Americans are talking about before Halloween, but they do not predict the exact candy preferred on every street. For trick-or-treat planning, consider a mix of chocolate and fruity choices, ingredient labels and any known allergy needs.

The research is timely because Google Trends USA shows seasonal topics alongside breaking sports and consumer news. Readers should distinguish a researcher’s interpretation of Google data from Google itself endorsing a product.
