Nicki Minaj Joins Trump at Michigan Rally: What Happened in Port Huron

The rapper appeared alongside the president at a Michigan campaign event for Republican candidates ahead of the midterms.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 26, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not the reported event, people or teams. Photo: Wendy Maxwell / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Nicki Minaj appeared at Donald Trump's October 25 campaign rally in Port Huron, Michigan, and publicly reaffirmed her support for him.

PORT HURON, Mich., Oct. 26, 2026 — Rapper Nicki Minaj joined President Donald Trump onstage during a Republican campaign rally in Port Huron on Sunday, October 25. Her remarks in support of the president quickly drew attention as the 2026 midterm election campaign entered its final stretch.

Why Nicki Minaj appeared at the Michigan rally

The event was held in support of Republican candidates in Michigan, including Senate candidate Mike Rogers and gubernatorial candidate John James. Minaj's appearance brought a major entertainment figure into a political setting at a time when both parties are seeking to motivate supporters ahead of November's vote.

Reporting from the rally described Minaj reaffirming her support for Trump and speaking positively about his leadership. Her comments should be understood as the artist's own political views, not as a statement of fact about policy outcomes or a reflection of the views of all her fans.

What she said onstage

Minaj told the audience she was honored to work with the president and said she intended to continue supporting him. Trump welcomed the performer and praised her appearance. Some of the president's comments about her body drew additional media coverage and criticism.

Different news organizations emphasized different aspects of the event: some focused on Minaj's endorsement, while others highlighted Trump's remarks and the broader political rhetoric at the rally. The appearance itself and the setting are corroborated by multiple independent reports.

How the appearance fits into the 2026 midterms

Michigan is an important battleground for statewide races, and the Port Huron event was part of the effort to turn out Republican voters. Celebrity appearances can attract media attention, but they do not by themselves establish how an election will be decided.

Minaj has publicly expressed support for Trump before, so Sunday's appearance was a continuation of an existing political alignment rather than her first expression of support. It nevertheless renewed online searches connecting the rapper with the president and the Michigan campaign.

What is confirmed and what remains opinion

The event took place on October 25 in Port Huron, Minaj appeared onstage and spoke in support of Trump, and the president made remarks about her. Whether that appearance will change voting behavior is not established by the event or the reporting cited here.
