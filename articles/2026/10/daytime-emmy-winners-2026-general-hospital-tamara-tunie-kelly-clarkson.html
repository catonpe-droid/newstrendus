Daytime Emmy Winners 2026: General Hospital, Tamara Tunie and Kelly Clarkson

The Academy's official winners report separates the night's headline prizes from the broader list of daytime television honors.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 31, 2026 · Verified against the awarding body's results · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph of a generic trophy presentation, not an Emmy statuette or a photo from the October 30 ceremony. Photo: RDNE Stock project / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: General Hospital won Outstanding Daytime Drama Series at the October 30, 2026 Daytime Emmys. Tamara Tunie won lead actress for Beyond the Gates, and The Kelly Clarkson Show won Outstanding Daytime Talk Series, according to the Academy's official winners report.

The 53rd Annual Daytime Emmy Awards were held October 30 at the Hollywood Palladium in Los Angeles. The National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences (NATAS) has released its official category-by-category winners report. It provides a more reliable answer than social posts that combine nominations, earlier ceremonies and this year's results.

Who won the biggest 2026 Daytime Emmy awards?

Outstanding Daytime Drama Series: General Hospital (ABC). Outstanding Daytime Talk Series: The Kelly Clarkson Show (NBCUniversal Syndication Studios). Both appear at the start of NATAS's official winners report. The distinction matters: a series award recognizes the program, not automatically every performer on its cast.

Lead actress in a daytime drama: Tamara Tunie, for playing Anita Williams Dupree on CBS's Beyond the Gates. Lead actor: Scott Clifton, for playing Liam Spencer on CBS's The Bold and the Beautiful. The Academy lists these as separate acting categories.

Which General Hospital and Beyond the Gates performers won?

Beyond the top series award, General Hospital was recognized for its daytime drama writing team. Jeff Kober won Outstanding Guest Performance for his role as Cyrus Renault. These are separate categories; readers should not confuse a guest-performance win with the lead-actor prize, which went to Clifton.

Beyond the Gates also earned the Outstanding Emerging Talent honor for Ambyr Michelle as Eva Thomas. The show was additionally named in the Academy's lighting-direction and hairstyling-and-makeup categories. Its acting success is significant for a relatively new CBS daytime drama competing alongside long-established programs.

Who won supporting actor, supporting actress and talk-show host?

The Young and the Restless won both supporting acting categories: Beth Maitland for Traci Abbott and Roger Howarth for Matt Clark. Sherri Shepherd won Outstanding Daytime Talk Series Host for Sherri, while The Kelly Clarkson Show took the program-level talk-series award. Those are different awards and should not be reported as one result.

Other winners in the official report include Ina Garten for Outstanding Culinary Host on Be My Guest with Ina Garten and Solo Traveling with Tracee Ellis Ross for Outstanding Travel and Adventure Program. This wider list demonstrates that the Daytime Emmys cover more than scripted soap operas.

What did the 2026 Daytime Emmys recognize, and what happens next?

NATAS's eligibility rules covered daytime programming from January 1 through December 31, 2025, even though the awards ceremony took place in October 2026. That distinction explains why a 2026 awards headline can honor work released during the previous calendar year. The Academy's entry and eligibility information documents the period.

The official winners document is organized by category and includes technical and production awards, so readers seeking a less prominent winner should consult the complete report rather than assume an abbreviated news list is exhaustive. The Academy's Daytime Emmy hub also links to ceremony coverage and the winners report.

Where can viewers check the complete official winners list?

The definitive reference is NATAS's 53rd Annual Daytime Emmy Awards winners PDF. Independent accounts from Associated Press and TV Insider corroborate the leading drama and acting results. Some news summaries count awards across different ceremony components; this article prioritizes named category winners in the Academy's primary document rather than a disputed total.
