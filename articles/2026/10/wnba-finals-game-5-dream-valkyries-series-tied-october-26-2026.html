WNBA Finals Game 5: Dream and Valkyries Meet With Series Tied 2–2

The 2026 WNBA Finals reached Game 5 on Monday, October 26, with the Atlanta Dream and Golden State Valkyries tied at two wins apiece after a tense four-game opening stretch.

At a glance: The best-of-seven championship series was level 2–2 entering Game 5 in California. Golden State won Games 1 and 4; Atlanta won Games 2 and 3. This report does not claim a Game 5 result.

Why Game 5 matters in the 2026 WNBA Finals

SAN FRANCISCO, Oct. 26, 2026 — After four games, neither the Atlanta Dream nor the Golden State Valkyries had established control of the championship series. Each side had won twice, meaning Monday's Game 5 would guarantee one team a 3–2 advantage in the best-of-seven Finals.

The WNBA's official Game 5 page lists Atlanta at Golden State. The matchup drew elevated U.S. search interest on Google Trends, but interest alone does not establish an outcome. At the time of writing, NewsTrendus has not independently verified a Game 5 final score and therefore does not present one.

How the Dream and Valkyries reached 2–2

Golden State opened with a 68–64 win on October 17. Atlanta answered 79–65 on October 19 and then won 97–71 at home on October 21, taking a 2–1 lead. Golden State's 67–62 victory on October 24 restored parity before the series returned to California. The series game log records those four completed results.

The pattern is revealing: three of the four games were decided by 14 points or fewer, while Atlanta's Game 3 win was the outlier. Momentum has swung, and neither team's earlier victory guarantees the next result.

Defense has shaped the matchup

In Game 1, Golden State held Atlanta to eight fourth-quarter points in a 68–64 win. Atlanta responded in Game 2 with a 79–65 victory behind Angel Reese's 30 points and 16 rebounds, according to San Francisco Chronicle reporting. Atlanta's Game 3 defense forced the Valkyries into a difficult offensive night, as described in The Guardian's account.

Game 4 showed the series can swing again when the scoring slows. Golden State's 67–62 win ensured the Finals would continue beyond Monday regardless of the Game 5 result.

Remaining WNBA Finals schedule and what to check

Game 5 was scheduled for October 26 at Golden State, with Game 6 scheduled for Thursday, October 29 in Atlanta. If the teams split Games 5 and 6, a deciding Game 7 would be required. Fans should use the official WNBA playoffs hub for any schedule or broadcast updates, because postseason timing can change.

The most meaningful Game 5 questions are whether Golden State can use its home court and defense to control tempo, and whether Atlanta can reproduce the interior production that fueled its earlier wins. Those are matchup considerations, not a prediction of the winner.
