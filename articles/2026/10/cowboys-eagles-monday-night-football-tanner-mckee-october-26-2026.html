Cowboys vs. Eagles Monday Night Football: Kickoff, Tanner McKee and NFC East Stakes

The Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles met on Monday Night Football on October 26 with both clubs seeking traction in the NFC East and Philadelphia facing uncertainty at quarterback.

Cowboys vs. Eagles: when and where was kickoff?

PHILADELPHIA, Oct. 26, 2026 — Dallas was scheduled to visit Philadelphia at Lincoln Financial Field on Monday night, with an 8:15 p.m. Eastern kickoff. The NFL's Eagles schedule lists the Week 7 matchup on ESPN, while The Guardian's viewing guide also identifies ABC coverage.

The rivalry drew heavy search interest in the United States. Because the game was scheduled for Monday night, live scores and lineup changes can move faster than a static article. This report deliberately does not publish a winner or final score without verification.

Why Tanner McKee is in the spotlight

Philadelphia entered the week with quarterback uncertainty after reports that Jalen Hurts was dealing with a serious health issue. Backup Tanner McKee consequently attracted attention as fans searched for the Eagles' starting quarterback and recent performance. The Guardian reported that McKee struggled in Philadelphia's previous 25–14 loss to Carolina.

A pregame report is not a substitute for the team's official inactive list or the final starting lineup. Readers should consult the Eagles' official team updates for confirmed availability rather than treating speculation as a medical diagnosis.

Both NFC East teams entered with 2–4 records

The NFL's Dallas schedule showed the Cowboys at 2–4 before the matchup, and Philadelphia was also 2–4. Dallas had lost 23–13 to Green Bay in Week 6; Philadelphia fell 25–14 to Carolina. That made Monday's meeting consequential for teams trying to stay competitive in their division.

It is too early to describe the result as a playoff elimination game. But a win would move one side closer to .500 and leave the other with a deeper hole to climb out of.

Matchup questions: protection, pressure and possession

Dallas quarterback Dak Prescott faced a Philadelphia defense coordinated by Vic Fangio, creating a tactical question about pressure and protection. The Guardian's pregame account highlighted Prescott's recent difficulties against blitzes. Philadelphia, meanwhile, needed reliable quarterback play and manageable third downs to avoid placing excessive pressure on its defense.

Turnovers and field position are likely to be especially important when two teams are trying to reverse losing runs. Those are general matchup considerations, not claims that a specific turnover or scoring play occurred in Monday's game.
