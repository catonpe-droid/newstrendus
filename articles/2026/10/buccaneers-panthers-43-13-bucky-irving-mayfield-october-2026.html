Buccaneers Crush Panthers 43–13: Bucky Irving Runs for Two TDs as Mayfield Returns

Tampa Bay broke through for a 30-point road victory at Carolina, using a relentless rushing attack, takeaways and special-teams plays to control Week 7.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 26, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: David Morris / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

Quick answer: The Buccaneers defeated the Panthers 43–13 on Sunday, October 25. Bucky Irving ran for 106 yards and two touchdowns; Tampa Bay amassed 258 rushing yards.

CHARLOTTE, N.C., Oct. 26, 2026 — The Tampa Bay Buccaneers beat the Carolina Panthers 43–13 on Sunday, October 25, turning a difficult start to their season into a decisive NFC South road win. The Buccaneers' official recap credits the result to 258 rushing yards, five sacks and a succession of takeaways that kept Carolina under pressure throughout the game.

Buccaneers vs. Panthers final score: how Tampa Bay built its lead

Tampa Bay scored on its opening drive when Bucky Irving finished a 70-yard possession with a four-yard touchdown run. Special teams then recovered a muffed punt to give the offense another short field. The bigger swing came in the second quarter: Al-Quadin Muhammad forced a fumble on a sack of Bryce Young, and Calijah Kancey returned the ball 32 yards for a defensive touchdown.

Carolina answered with a 50-yard touchdown pass from Young to Darren Waller, but even that sequence went badly for the hosts. Tampa Bay blocked the extra-point attempt and Zyon McCollum returned it for two points. By halftime, the Buccaneers led 33–6, a gap Carolina never threatened to erase.

Bucky Irving and the run game powered the victory

Irving finished with 106 yards on 24 carries and two rushing touchdowns. His patient running behind the offensive line helped Tampa Bay stay ahead of the chains and maintain possession. Sean Tucker supplied the explosive play later, breaking a 46-yard touchdown run in the fourth quarter. The team finished with 258 yards on the ground, a figure that explains why it could win convincingly without asking its quarterback to throw frequently.

Baker Mayfield returned after missing three games with a right-thumb injury. He completed seven of 11 passes for 64 yards and one touchdown, including a scoring throw to Tez Johnson. The performance was not about passing volume; the offense used its ground advantage to control field position and scoring opportunities.

Defense and special teams made it a complete win

Yaya Diaby recorded three sacks, according to the team's rapid reaction. Tampa Bay's defense intercepted Young twice and turned a strip-sack into a touchdown. Young finished 14-of-27 for 163 yards with one passing touchdown and two interceptions. The Panthers struggled to sustain drives, particularly while the margin was growing in the first half.

The result improved Tampa Bay to 2–5 and left Carolina at 3–3. The win is a meaningful response for the Buccaneers, but one afternoon does not erase the losses that preceded it.

What's next for Tampa Bay?

The Buccaneers' team schedule lists a home game against the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday, November 1. The central question is whether the balanced rushing and defensive production from Charlotte can be repeated against another divisional opponent. For Carolina, ball security and pass protection stand out as immediate priorities after the defeat.
