Is In-N-Out Coming to Oklahoma, Arkansas or Mississippi? What the CEO Actually Said

In-N-Out's owner identified Oklahoma, Arkansas and Mississippi as possible expansion markets, not confirmed store openings. New Mexico is separately expected to receive its first location in 2027.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 30, 2026 · Independently written from linked sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph of an existing In-N-Out sign; not a new location announcement. Photo: Naveen Ketterer / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: In-N-Out's owner identified Oklahoma, Arkansas and Mississippi as possible expansion markets, not confirmed store openings. New Mexico is separately expected to receive its first location in 2027.

A new In-N-Out Burger in Oklahoma, Arkansas or Mississippi is a possibility—not a scheduled opening. Owner and president Lynsi Snyder-Ellingson identified the three states while discussing the chain's measured growth with actor Terry Crews in an interview released for its 78th anniversary. She did not give a launch date, specific city, construction site or confirmed restaurant address for any of them.

Which three states did Lynsi Snyder mention?

During the official In-N-Out conversation with Terry Crews, Snyder discussed the company's distribution footprint and mentioned Arkansas, Mississippi and Oklahoma as possible markets. The important distinction is between a state being under consideration and an actual store announcement. As of the October 30 reporting reviewed here, no opening date or address for those three states was established.

Reporting by Patch in Oklahoma and Parade independently describes the same three possibilities. Neither report supplies a confirmed Oklahoma, Arkansas or Mississippi store opening. Search results and social posts that turn the possibility into a promised opening overstate what Snyder said.

What is confirmed about In-N-Out's next state?

New Mexico is a different case. Snyder said the company is preparing to enter the state, with its first locations expected in 2027. The October interview also discussed In-N-Out's entry into Tennessee, where the chain opened restaurants in 2025. Those plans should not be confused with the three additional states Snyder merely floated.

The company's official locations page is the most practical place for customers to verify an actual open restaurant. A future market mention does not mean that a location is accepting customers, hiring at a particular address or taking orders.

Why is expansion slower than at other burger chains?

In-N-Out says its growth model depends on delivering fresh food through its own distribution network. The company has historically avoided frozen beef and seeks to keep restaurants within a workable supply distance. That explains why the existing Texas-to-Tennessee corridor matters when assessing Arkansas, Mississippi and Oklahoma.

Snyder also described the importance of maintaining restaurant cleanliness, service and consistency as the chain grows. This is not simply a question of consumer demand: a proposed state must fit the operating model. In the interview she rejected the idea that In-N-Out is trying to open restaurants everywhere at once.

Is In-N-Out leaving California?

No. Snyder reiterated that the chain remains committed to California, its original home, despite her family's move toward Tennessee and the company's eastern operations. Previous rumors that all California restaurants would close were not supported by the company's statements. The San Francisco Chronicle previously reported the chain's explicit rejection of that claim.

How can customers check a real opening announcement?

For Oklahoma, Arkansas and Mississippi, look for an official location listing, a city-specific announcement or a documented store opening date—not a viral headline about states that are only being considered. In-N-Out's location finder is the primary reference for operating restaurants. An announcement about a distribution center or regional office does not by itself establish a restaurant opening.
