Suns Beat Warriors 131–122: Devin Booker Scores 43 in Phoenix Win

Devin Booker's 43-point night gave Phoenix a home-opening victory, while Golden State's winless start continued amid several absences.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Based on verified source reporting · Representative editorial photograph

Quick answer: Devin Booker's 43-point night gave Phoenix a home-opening victory, while Golden State's winless start continued amid several absences.

PHOENIX, Arizona — Devin Booker scored 43 points to lead the Phoenix Suns to a 131–122 victory over the Golden State Warriors on Saturday, October 24. Phoenix recovered from a poor season-opening defeat and claimed its home opener, while Golden State dropped to 0–3. The final score is confirmed by the NBA's official game records and independent game reports.

Warriors vs Suns: How Phoenix pulled ahead

The Suns went into halftime with a slim 57–54 advantage after an uneven start. Booker helped widen that gap in the third quarter, scoring 17 points in the period. By the end of the third, Phoenix led 102–92. That gave the home team room to absorb a late Warriors push without losing control of the contest.

Booker scored efficiently, making 17 of 25 field-goal attempts. He also registered seven assists and seven rebounds, a reminder that the performance was not only about scoring volume. Phoenix used the third quarter to build the separation that its opening quarter had not produced.

Why Golden State was short-handed

Several regular Golden State contributors did not play. Stephen Curry was sidelined with a right-knee issue, while Brandin Podziemski, Draymond Green and Al Horford also missed the game with reported back problems. The Warriors were playing their third game in four days, adding another challenge to an early-season road test.

The missing players are important context for the 0–3 record, but the result still counts. Golden State needs wins regardless of its rotation. The game was also an opportunity for less-established players to take meaningful offensive responsibility.

Who stepped up for the Warriors?

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg led Golden State with 26 points and made six three-pointers. De'Anthony Melton added 25 points. Those numbers gave the visitors a path to compete even without some of their regular offensive options.

For Phoenix, Miles Bridges contributed 16 points and Dillon Brooks added 15. Luke Kennard provided outside shooting in the fourth quarter, helping stabilize the home side when the Warriors remained within reach. Those supporting contributions mattered because Booker could not create every shot himself.

What comes next for both teams

The result gave Phoenix a response after a 124–83 loss to Portland in its opener. For Golden State, a winless start is an early warning rather than a final verdict on the season. Availability updates for injured or rested players will be as important as the next scoreline.

The NBA and team schedules listed Golden State at Denver and Phoenix at Oklahoma City on Monday. Game status and injury designations can change, so check official team pages close to tipoff. This article records Saturday's completed result rather than a live scoreboard.
