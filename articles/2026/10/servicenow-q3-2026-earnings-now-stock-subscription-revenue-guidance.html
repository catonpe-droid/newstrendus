ServiceNow Q3 2026 Earnings: $4.1B Revenue, AI Outlook and Why NOW Stock Fell

Strong subscription sales and a higher AI contract-value target met a cautious market response to future contracted-revenue growth.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 29, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not a photo of the reported event or the exact product or person. Photo: panumas nikhomkhai / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: ServiceNow reported $4.106 billion in Q3 2026 revenue, 21% subscription growth and $13.65 billion in cRPO. What its Q4 guidance means for NOW stock.

ServiceNow's third-quarter 2026 earnings showed growing enterprise software demand alongside a more complicated outlook for investors. The company reported $4.106 billion in revenue for the quarter ended September 30, up 20.5% from a year earlier, and subscription revenue of $3.988 billion, up 21%, according to its October 28 earnings release. Yet NOW shares fell sharply in after-hours trading as investors assessed the next quarter's contracted-revenue outlook.

What did ServiceNow report in Q3 2026?

Subscription revenue made up the vast majority of quarterly sales. The company also disclosed $13.65 billion in current remaining performance obligations, or cRPO, up 20.5% year over year. That measure represents contracted revenue expected to be recognized over the next 12 months; it is not the same as revenue already booked in the quarter.

Total remaining performance obligations reached $29.7 billion, an increase of 22%. ServiceNow recorded $277 million in GAAP net income, or $0.27 per diluted share. On a non-GAAP basis, it reported $1.00 per diluted share. These two earnings figures use different accounting adjustments and should not be compared as interchangeable measures.

Why did NOW stock fall despite higher revenue?

Independent market coverage reported a decline of more than 17% in extended trading after the results. One focus was the company's 18.5% fourth-quarter cRPO growth guidance, which was below some analysts' expectations even though reported third-quarter growth remained above 20%. Stock-price reactions can also reflect valuation, profit expectations and broader technology-sector sentiment; a single forecast number does not establish the sole cause.

Investors looking at NOW stock should distinguish a post-earnings price reaction from a change in the underlying reported sales. ServiceNow's revenue grew, while the market debated how quickly future contracted demand would expand.

What is ServiceNow's Q4 2026 guidance?

The company projected fourth-quarter subscription revenue of $4.245 billion to $4.255 billion, representing 22.5% to 23% year-over-year growth under its GAAP outlook. It also forecast an approximately 32% non-GAAP operating margin for the quarter. The cRPO outlook is a separate measure of near-term contractual commitments, not a direct projection of quarterly recognized revenue.

How big is the AI business?

ServiceNow raised its 2026 target for annual contract value associated with AI offerings to $1.7 billion, up from a $1 billion target at the beginning of the year. Management also highlighted broader enterprise-agent and workflow initiatives. Annual contract value is a sales metric, however, not the same as revenue recognized immediately or cash received.

The company increased its full-year subscription revenue guidance to $15.780 billion–$15.790 billion and its targeted free-cash-flow margin to 36%. Those are forward-looking company estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.

What should U.S. investors watch next?

Key follow-ups include the next quarterly cRPO disclosure, conversion of AI contract value into recurring revenue, profitability after acquisition costs, and the difference between GAAP and adjusted earnings. ServiceNow's results illustrate a common tension in enterprise AI investing: growth can be robust while a premium share price remains sensitive to any sign of slowing forward demand.
