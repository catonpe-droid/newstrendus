Jay-Z to Headline Super Bowl 2027 Halftime Show: Date, TV Channels and Roc Nation Role

The artist whose company has helped shape the NFL's halftime show since 2019 is now its announced headliner. Here's what is confirmed for viewers.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 27, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Caleb Oquendo / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Jay-Z is the confirmed Super Bowl LXI halftime headliner on February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The NFL, Apple Music and Roc Nation announced the performance on October 26, 2026. The official release names ESPN, ABC, Disney+ and NFL+ among viewing options.

When and where is Jay-Z performing at Super Bowl LXI?

LOS ANGELES, Oct. 27, 2026 — Jay-Z will headline the Apple Music Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show on Sunday, February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The news comes from a joint announcement by the NFL, Apple Music and Roc Nation issued late October 26. This is a confirmed booking, not a rumored guest appearance.

Jay-Z, born Shawn Carter, has worked on the business and creative side of the halftime production for years. His move from choosing and helping produce performers to taking center stage is the key distinction behind this announcement. The league's game date and location were established before the artist reveal.

Where can U.S. viewers watch the 2027 halftime show?

The official announcement identifies ESPN, ABC and Disney+ for the game and halftime broadcast, with mobile access through NFL+. It also identifies ESPN Deportes and a separate Univision Spanish-language presentation. Availability can depend on a viewer's subscription, local market and service; the announcement does not make every platform free.

The halftime performance occurs during the game, not at a separately fixed clock time. Viewers should use the NFL's final game-day schedule for kickoff and plan to tune in before the second quarter ends rather than rely on an unconfirmed exact halftime start time.

Why is Jay-Z's Roc Nation connection important?

Roc Nation has collaborated with the NFL on live music and entertainment since 2019. That role has put Jay-Z behind several high-profile halftime productions while he remained off the stage as a headline performer. The Associated Press reports that NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell had long wanted Jay-Z to perform when the timing suited him.

That makes the 2027 choice unusual: a longtime creative partner is also the announced featured artist. The selection does not, by itself, reveal the full set list, guest singers, staging, or how long the musical segment will run. Those details should not be presented as decided until organizers publish them.

What music and career milestones could shape the show?

Jay-Z's catalogue spans Reasonable Doubt, The Blueprint and The Black Album, as well as songs such as “99 Problems” and “Empire State of Mind.” The 2026 announcement arrives during the 30th-anniversary celebration of his 1996 debut. He is a 25-time Grammy winner and a member of the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.

Those milestones help explain the interest in the booking, but no specific track or surprise guest is confirmed in the announcement. Claims that Beyoncé, Alicia Keys or another artist is booked to join him should be treated as speculation unless separately verified.

What is confirmed—and what remains to be announced?

The headliner, date, venue and named U.S. broadcasters are confirmed by the organizing partners. Reuters independently reported the selection on October 27. No official song list or supporting-artist lineup was supplied with the release. This article uses a generic concert-crowd photograph; it is not an image of Jay-Z or a rehearsal.
