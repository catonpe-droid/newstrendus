Samsung News Shutdown: December 21 Date and What U.S. Galaxy Users Should Know

The reported Samsung News closure date is real for markets receiving the notice, but U.S. readers should not assume the same deadline applies to their Galaxy phones.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 31, 2026 · Independently written from linked sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not a photo of the reported event or named people/products. Photo: RDNE Stock project / Pexels (free-use license).

Quick answer: An in-app Samsung News notice shown to users in India and South Korea specifies December 21, 2026, at 00:00 UTC as a shutdown time. Public reports reviewed here do not establish an identical end date for every U.S. Galaxy user.

UNITED STATES — “Samsung News app discontinued 2026” has become a rising search query, but the regional details matter. A notice displayed inside the Samsung News app in India and South Korea says that content delivery will end on December 21, 2026, at 00:00 UTC, according to SamMobile. That timing should not be presented as a confirmed worldwide rollout schedule.

What will stop working where the notice applies?

Samsung News combines headlines and topic feeds in a standalone app, a home-screen widget and, on supported devices, a leftmost home-screen feed. The notice says that the widget will no longer receive content updates after the service ends. Users in affected markets are advised to remove the widget from their home screens once it no longer serves a purpose.

The notice, as reported, also addresses associated profiles and personalization data. Samsung says it will delete information collected for the service when it ends, subject to any retention required by local law. That is a reported service-specific plan, not a guarantee about all data associated with a Samsung account.

Is Samsung News shutting down in the United States on December 21?

A U.S.-specific cutoff has not been independently confirmed in the sources reviewed for this article. Reports reproduce notices from India and South Korea. They do not establish that every American Galaxy phone or tablet received the same notice or that its U.S. news feed will disappear on the same day.

Android Authority describes the discontinuation and expected changes to the One UI home-screen experience. Some reporting speculates that Google Discover may become the remaining default news feed in affected configurations, but exact replacement behavior may depend on device, software and region. Samsung has not provided a complete replacement timetable in the cited reports.

How can Galaxy owners check their own device?

Open the Samsung News app, if installed, and read any official in-app notice. Check the phone’s software and regional availability rather than relying on an overseas screenshot. If the widget eventually stops updating, remove it using your phone’s home-screen customization controls; removal of a widget is not the same as deletion of all phone data.

For an alternative feed, check whether Google Discover is offered on your particular Galaxy model and launcher. There is no basis in the cited notices to claim that a U.S. user must download an unfamiliar third-party “Samsung News replacement” app or pay a fee to keep reading headlines.

Why is the regional distinction important?

The December date is precise, but its documented distribution is limited. A reader in New York needs a different answer from a reader in Seoul who has seen an official notice. Treat announcements about a universal U.S. deadline, automatic account deletion across Samsung services or an official replacement app as unverified until Samsung provides broader documentation.
