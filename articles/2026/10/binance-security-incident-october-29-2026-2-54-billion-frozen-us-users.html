Binance Security Incident: $2.54B Frozen, What U.S. Crypto Users Should Know

Binance is investigating a potential security breach as partners freeze $2.54 billion. What is confirmed, what is not and how Binance.US differs.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 29, 2026 (U.S. Eastern) · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

At a glance: Binance is investigating a potential security breach as partners freeze $2.54 billion. What is confirmed, what is not and how Binance.US differs.

What did Binance confirm on October 29?

Binance said Thursday, October 29, 2026, that it was investigating a potential security issue. The global cryptocurrency exchange told journalists that its security teams were urgently reviewing reports and maintained that customer funds remained safe. It temporarily suspended withdrawals on multiple networks as a precaution, according to TechCrunch's reporting on the company statement. Founder Changpeng Zhao, who no longer runs the exchange, said the incident appeared to be a hack. That description is not a final forensic determination.

What does $2.54 billion in frozen crypto mean?

Binance said industry partners had frozen a verified $2.54 billion in assets linked to the investigation by Thursday evening. Reuters reported that Tether had frozen nearly $1.57 billion and Circle more than $800 million. The amount could change as investigators and issuers traced further transactions. Importantly, a freeze does not itself establish that the entire amount was stolen from customers. The Associated Press also distinguished confirmed freezes from unverified reports of possible losses.

How much money was actually stolen?

No definitive total of verified stolen assets had been publicly established in the cited reports at publication. Some blockchain observers offered much larger estimates, but those figures should not be presented as proven customer losses. Nor does a short-term Bitcoin price decline reveal how a breach occurred or what the ultimate damage will be. The questions that remain open include the entry point, the affected wallets, whether customers incurred losses and which funds can be recovered.

Was Binance.US affected?

Binance.US is a separate U.S.-serving exchange, not simply another name for the global Binance.com platform. Reuters reported that Binance.US said it was not affected by the October 29 incident. That is a statement about its condition at the time, not a guarantee against future disruptions. Customers should confirm which company operates their account and consult that platform's current official service notices.

What should crypto customers do now?

Use the official app or a known website address to review account status, service restrictions and company notices. Ignore unsolicited offers of a refund, emergency wallet transfer or privileged recovery link; never disclose seed phrases, passwords or authentication codes. Preserve copies of legitimate account statements and official notices. It is too early to treat the security incident as fully resolved or to accept speculative loss estimates. A detailed company post-incident report and independent verification will be needed to settle the outstanding questions.
