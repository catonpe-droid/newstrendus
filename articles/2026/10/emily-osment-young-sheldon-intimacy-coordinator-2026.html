Why Emily Osment Requested an Intimacy Coordinator on Young Sheldon

A new oral history of Young Sheldon explains Emily Osment's request for an intimacy coordinator during early scenes with Montana Jordan.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 25, 2026 · Independently written from credited reporting · Representative editorial photograph

Representative editorial photograph, not an image of the reported event or people. Photo: Cemrecan Yurtman / Pexels (free-use license).

Quick answer: A new Young Sheldon oral history describes why Osment asked for a coordinator while filming scenes with then-18-year-old co-star Montana Jordan.

TELEVISION — Emily Osment has explained why she requested an intimacy coordinator while filming early romantic scenes with Montana Jordan on Young Sheldon. The account appears in Jessica Radloff's new oral history of the CBS sitcom and drew fresh attention on October 24, 2026, as entertainment outlets discussed the behind-the-scenes decision.

What Emily Osment said about filming with Montana Jordan

Osment joined Young Sheldon as Mandy McAllister, whose storyline paired her with Georgie Cooper, played by Jordan. According to reporting based on Radloff's book, Osment was 30 when Jordan was 18 during the early scenes in question. She said she wanted the production to consider his comfort and ability to request support, not simply her own experience as an actor.

The actress described raising the issue with producer Tim Marx. A coordinator could not be arranged immediately for the first filming day, but one was brought in for later episodes. Osment said the additional support made the scenes easier to navigate.

What is an intimacy coordinator?

An intimacy coordinator helps a production plan and communicate the boundaries of scenes involving physical closeness. That can include discussing choreography, consent, rehearsal conditions and the practical needs of performers and crew. The role is not limited to explicit sexual scenes; kissing or other close-contact scenes can also benefit from clear agreements.

That distinction is central to this story. The production's concern was not that a scripted romance was inherently improper. It was that performers had different levels of experience and a significant age difference, and that both should be able to participate in a comfortable, clearly communicated process.

How the show's team responded

Director Alex Reid acknowledged in the book that the team had not planned for an intimacy coordinator at first, partly because the series did not contain sex scenes. Co-creator Steve Molaro said Osment was justified in making the request. Later filming incorporated the additional support, according to the accounts reported by TheWrap and Entertainment Weekly.

These comments describe a production-process change rather than an allegation of misconduct by Jordan. There is no basis in the cited reporting to present the younger actor as having done anything wrong.

Where are Georgie and Mandy now?

Young Sheldon concluded in 2024 after seven seasons. Osment and Jordan continued their characters' story in the spinoff Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage. The new oral history has renewed interest in how the original show's creative team handled the relationship's on-screen age difference.

For viewers, the broader takeaway is a behind-the-scenes lesson: even on a network comedy without explicit scenes, planning and clear communication can improve the working environment for performers.

Frequently asked questions

Why did Emily Osment ask for an intimacy coordinator?

She said she wanted additional support for romantic scenes with then-18-year-old co-star Montana Jordan and wanted him to have the option of requesting that support.

Did Young Sheldon use an intimacy coordinator afterward?

Yes. Reporting from the new oral history says one attended subsequent filming.

Are Emily Osment and Montana Jordan still co-stars?

Yes. They continued their fictional characters in Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage.
