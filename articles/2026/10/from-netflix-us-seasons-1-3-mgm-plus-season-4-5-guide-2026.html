Is From on Netflix US? Seasons Available, MGM+ and the Season 5 Outlook

The horror mystery's first three seasons reached a wider streaming audience ahead of Halloween. New viewers should know where the later episodes live.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 27, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event or people. Photo: Helen Mur / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Industry reporting says seasons 1–3 of the MGM+ horror series From became available on Netflix US on October 19, 2026. Later episodes remain associated with MGM+, and season 5 is expected in 2027; no exact season-five premiere date is confirmed here.

Is From available on Netflix in the United States?

NEW YORK, Oct. 27, 2026 — The supernatural mystery series From has found a larger U.S. streaming audience in time for Halloween. The first three seasons were scheduled to arrive on Netflix US on October 19, 2026, according to specialist streaming coverage by What's on Netflix and reporting from Forbes.

The series began on Epix, which became MGM+, and remains associated with that service. Netflix's title listing identifies the show, its horror premise and lead cast. Streaming rights are territorial and can change; viewers should check their own U.S. account rather than assume an overseas listing applies everywhere.

Where can viewers watch From season 4?

The October Netflix addition was reported as seasons 1 through 3, not a full-series transfer. Viewers who finish those episodes and want to continue into season 4 should check MGM+ or its supported subscription channels. A Netflix listing for earlier seasons does not mean the newest season is included.

Decider's October 20 viewing guide makes the same distinction. Plans, trial terms and channel bundles can change; any claim that all episodes are free on one service should be verified at the time of viewing.

What is From about, without spoilers?

Created by John Griffin, From follows travelers trapped in a seemingly ordinary town they cannot leave. Dangerous forces emerge after dark, and residents try to survive while piecing together the rules of their environment. Harold Perrineau leads an ensemble that includes Catalina Sandino Moreno and Eion Bailey.

The premise blends survival horror with an ongoing puzzle. New viewers should expect unanswered questions, interconnected character stories and a serial narrative rather than a stand-alone mystery resolved every episode. It is best to start at season 1, episode 1; jumping to the later seasons can spoil major revelations.

Is From season 5 confirmed, and when will it premiere?

Horror-industry reporting indicates that a fifth season is expected in 2027. Bloody Disgusting reported the season-five production outlook while covering the October Netflix licensing window. The coverage describes it as the final season, but this article does not claim a precise premiere day or episode count.

That distinction matters for searches such as “From season 5 release date.” An expected year is not an official dated premiere. The series' original network and producers are the appropriate sources for subsequent scheduling changes, trailers and distribution updates.

What should U.S. viewers check before subscribing?

Confirm three things in the app: whether seasons 1–3 appear on your Netflix profile, whether MGM+ carries season 4 in your location, and the current subscription or trial terms. Streaming libraries can vary by region and plan, and licensing windows can expire.
