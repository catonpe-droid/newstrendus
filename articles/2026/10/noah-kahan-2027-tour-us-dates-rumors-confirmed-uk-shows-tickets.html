Noah Kahan 2027 Tour: Confirmed Dates, U.S. Rumors and Ticket Updates

A wave of North American tour speculation has fans searching for dates, but the artist's published calendar is the key source for confirmed concerts.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · October 29, 2026 · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

Representative licensed photograph, not the reported event, product or person. Photo: Caleb Oquendo / Pexels (Pexels free-use license).

At a glance: Noah Kahan 2027 tour searches are rising. Check confirmed UK and Ireland stadium dates, what is unannounced for the US and how to avoid fake ticket listings.

Search interest in a Noah Kahan 2027 tour has surged as fans look for a possible North American stadium announcement. As of the October 28 U.S. evening news cycle, Kahan's official tour calendar confirms 2027 concerts in the United Kingdom and Ireland, but the sources reviewed do not establish a newly announced list of 2027 U.S. stadium dates.

Which Noah Kahan 2027 dates are actually confirmed?

The artist's official tour page lists July 13, 2027, at Hampden Park in Glasgow; July 17 and 18 at Aviva Stadium in Dublin; and July 23, 24 and 25 at Wembley Stadium in London. The page distinguishes ticket availability from sold-out shows and waitlists, which can change after publication.

Ticketmaster's U.K. editorial page separately documented the addition of a second Wembley date in August 2026. The official artist calendar now lists three Wembley dates, so a months-old ticket article should not be treated as the complete current schedule. Fans should verify each venue and date at the time of purchase.

Has a 2027 U.S. tour been announced?

Not in the official tour listings reviewed for this report. Entertainment outlets have described cryptic venue posts and suggested that an announcement could arrive October 29, but speculation is not a confirmed routing, ticket sale or presale. A venue teaser may signal an upcoming event without proving the artist, date or price.

A careful distinction is essential: confirmed overseas shows are real, while a specific U.S. city list or presale password circulating without an artist, venue or authorized ticketing announcement remains unverified. This report will not invent dates to fill that gap.

What is The Last of the Bugs World Tour?

The announced 2027 stadium dates are marketed under The Last of the Bugs World Tour name. Kahan's continuing popularity followed the success of Stick Season and The Great Divide era, making larger venues a plausible next step, but commercial expectations should not be mistaken for a confirmed U.S. itinerary.

His current schedule also shows November and December 2026 performances in Europe, followed by a January 2027 festival in Mexico. Those are separate dates and should not be relabeled as U.S. tour stops.

Where should fans check for legitimate tickets?

Start with Kahan's own tour page and follow its links to official venue or ticketing partners. Confirm that the event name, venue, city, date and sale time agree across the artist and venue sites. If a resale marketplace lists an unannounced U.S. show, that listing alone does not establish that tickets have been issued.

Avoid paying for purported presale codes from strangers, entering payment information through unsolicited social-media links, or assuming a search advertisement is the artist's official website. Fees and resale rules vary by jurisdiction and platform.

What should U.S. fans watch next?

The most useful next update would be a dated announcement from Kahan, a named U.S. venue or an authorized ticket seller with a complete event listing. It should state the city, venue, show date, sale schedule and ticketing method. Until then, a verified list of 2027 U.S. dates cannot be supplied.

This is a verification guide, not a promise that a particular city will receive a concert. Information was checked against publicly available official listings and independent reports on October 28–29, 2026.
