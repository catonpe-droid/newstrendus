McDonald’s Q3 2026: US Same-Store Sales Slip as New Value Bundles Are Tested

McDonald’s US comparable sales fell 0.2% in Q3 2026. What the earnings report says and what to know about possible value bundles in 2027.

Quick answer: McDonald’s US comparable sales fell 0.2% in Q3 2026. What the earnings report says and what to know about possible value bundles in 2027.

What did McDonald's report for the third quarter?

McDonald's reported a 0.2% decline in U.S. comparable sales for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, according to its November 4 earnings release. Global comparable sales rose 1.5%. The company reported a 1.4% increase in its International Operated Markets segment and a 4.2% increase in International Developmental Licensed Markets.

The company's SEC filing described lower U.S. customer traffic as being largely offset by higher average spending per visit. That distinction matters: stronger average tickets do not necessarily mean a restaurant chain is serving more customers.

Which value meals are being tested?

Associated Press reporting described trials of mix-and-match meal combinations and fixed-price bundles in approximately 1,900 U.S. restaurants. Some franchisees also offer localized deals, so customers in different markets may see different menus and price points.

McDonald's signaled a possible early-2027 rollout of value-oriented bundles. A test is not the same as a confirmed nationwide product launch, and no universal menu price can be inferred from the trial.

Why are budget offers becoming more important?

Restaurant customers are comparing everyday food costs more closely. Quick-service chains compete on headline price, portion size, convenience and app promotions. Value bundles may attract visits by making the overall cost of a meal clearer, but they do not mean all menu items will become cheaper.

The company said it was encouraged by progress from its value initiatives. Whether the tests produce sustained traffic growth will require later sales reports, rather than assumptions from a single quarter.

What does the broader earnings report show?

McDonald's global sales picture differed from its U.S. segment. Consolidated revenue was up around 1% year over year, and reported diluted earnings per share were $3.51, or $3.57 on an adjusted basis under the company's stated adjustments.

These are company-reported figures for the historical quarter, not a guarantee about upcoming results or a recommendation concerning its shares.

How can customers check the offers?

Customers should verify current availability, price, terms and restaurant participation through local store menus or the official McDonald's app. Individual trials may be withdrawn, revised or expanded before any final rollout.
