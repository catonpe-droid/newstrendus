Kakgold RC Car Recall: Check the Toy and Get a Refund

A federal recall identifies a specific four-wheel-drive stunt car toy sold on Amazon. Families can compare the vehicle's distinctive appearance and follow the manufacturer's refund process.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 14, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

This is a source-linked consumer explainer, not firsthand product testing. AI-assisted drafting may be used; see our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photograph of a different RC off-road vehicle; it does not show the recalled Kakgold product. Photo: xinyu liu / Pexels.

Quick answer: On November 12, 2026, Kakgold recalled approximately 294 remote-controlled 4WD stunt car toys because batteries inside the controller are accessible to children. Stop using the affected toy and contact Kakgold at kakrecall@outlook.com for a full refund. The official CPSC notice, recall 27-085, includes product photos.

Which Kakgold RC car is recalled?

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission describes a stunt vehicle with a lime-green and blue body, black chassis and four black wheels with lime-green trim and diagonal rollers. The body has checkered graphics, an X motif and the words “PRO” and “RACING.” A white hang tag says “Do Not Twist the Shaft” in red. These details help identify the affected toy, but shoppers should compare their product directly with CPSC's actual recall photographs. The representative image above is not a product-identification image.

Why is the controller dangerous?

The remote control contains button-cell and lithium-ion batteries that children can access, violating the mandatory toy-safety standard. Swallowed button or coin batteries can cause severe internal chemical burns and potentially fatal injury. The agency reports no incidents or injuries associated with this recall as of its November 12 notice. That absence of reports is not a reason to continue using a recalled toy.

When and where was the toy sold?

CPSC says the affected Kakgold stunt vehicles were sold on Amazon.com from July through September 2026, priced between $34 and $50. The recall covers about 294 units. The retailer is Shenzhenshi Juteng Lechuang Technology Co., Ltd., doing business as Kakgold, based in China. An Amazon purchase history may help customers locate the relevant order; the official CPSC images are the most reliable visual reference.

How to request the Kakgold recall refund

First, stop using the recalled remote-controlled toy and keep it away from children. Contact kakrecall@outlook.com for the full-refund instructions. According to the federal recall notice, customers will be asked to mark the product “RECALLED” and email a photograph showing it properly discarded. Do not place loose button-cell or lithium-ion batteries into ordinary household trash: CPSC specifically says batteries should be disposed of or recycled in accordance with local hazardous-waste procedures. Follow the official instructions and local rules before disposal. NewsTrendus does not process claims or request personal purchase information.

What should families do if their toy looks similar?

Do not assume that every remote-controlled stunt car is included. Compare the color, wheel construction, graphics and hang tag with the official product photos. If the product is uncertain, ask the manufacturer rather than relying on a third-party picture. For a separate November 2026 children's product safety issue, see our SHEIN baby rattle recall guide.
