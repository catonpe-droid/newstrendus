Jim Jordan Wins House GOP Leader Vote 106–73

Ohio Republican Jim Jordan defeated Steve Scalise in a closed-door party vote, positioning himself to lead House Republicans when the new Congress convenes in January 2027.

Quick answer: House Republicans elected Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio as their leader for the next Congress on November 10, 2026. Jordan defeated Louisiana Rep. Steve Scalise 106 votes to 73 in a private conference ballot, according to the Associated Press and Reuters. The vote chooses the party's incoming leader; it does not immediately change who controls the current House.

Who won the House Republican leadership election?

Jordan received 106 votes to Scalise's 73, a 33-vote margin in the Republican conference election. The Associated Press reported the vote tally, while Reuters independently reported Jordan's selection. These are votes cast by Republican lawmakers and members-elect in a party leadership contest—not a national election or a floor vote by the full House.

When does Jim Jordan become minority leader?

The leadership choice concerns the Congress beginning January 3, 2027. Democrats won the House in the November 3 midterms, so Republicans are preparing to serve as the minority party. Jordan's selection does not make him Speaker of the House. The Speaker is selected by the full chamber after the new Congress convenes; the Democratic majority will have its own leadership and Speaker selection process.

Why did Republicans choose Jordan over Scalise?

Both candidates offered different arguments for rebuilding the party after its election losses. Jordan emphasized his experience challenging Democratic oversight efforts and his willingness to take a combative approach. Scalise pointed to his years in House leadership and campaign fundraising. Those were competing political pitches, not proof that one strategy will succeed. Jordan's official congressional website published his minority-leader pitch on November 4.

What changes for voters and Congress?

The minority leader organizes the party's floor strategy, coordinates messages and negotiations, and helps decide how members respond to the majority's legislative agenda. The position does not by itself give Republicans control over committee chairs or which bills reach a vote. With Democrats poised to hold the majority, Jordan will be an opposition leader rather than the chamber's presiding officer.

For U.S. readers tracking investigations, budget negotiations and legislation, the practical question is whether Republicans can influence votes or negotiations despite their smaller numbers. Jordan can shape his conference's response, but the eventual outcome depends on the incoming House's membership, procedural rules and negotiations with Democrats.

What happens next in January 2027?

The new House is scheduled to convene January 3. Members will elect a Speaker and adopt rules, while party leadership teams organize their priorities. Republicans also selected other conference officials on November 10; AP's election account identifies Tom Emmer as the incoming Republican whip. Readers should distinguish the completed November 10 internal party ballot from January's separate House-wide procedures.
