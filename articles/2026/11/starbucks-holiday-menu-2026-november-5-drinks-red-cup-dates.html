Starbucks Holiday Menu 2026 Starts Nov. 5: New Drinks, Red Cup Day and Bearista Dates

The holiday launch is today, but the cup collectibles arrive on different dates. This is a shopper-first breakdown of what is new, what returns and what Starbucks actually announced.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 5, 2026 · Primary-source and local-report review · Representative photograph

This is a source-linked desk explainer, not first-hand reporting. Research and drafting may use AI-assisted tools. Follow cited records and our editorial and corrections policy.

Quick guide: Starbucks’ 2026 U.S. holiday menu begins Thursday, November 5. New drinks include hot or iced Tiramisu Velvet Latte, Tiramisu Velvet Cold Brew and Apple Pomegranate Refreshers. Red Cup Day is listed for November 17, while separate Bearista merchandise drops are scheduled for November 10 and December 1.

Starbucks has officially launched the seasonal menu described in its November 4 menu announcement. The practical issue for customers is that the food-and-drink launch, limited collectibles and promotional cup giveaway do not all happen on the same day. The schedule below separates the confirmed dates to help customers avoid wasted trips or rumors about a different store’s inventory.

Which Starbucks holiday drinks are new in 2026?

The main new espresso-based lineup is Tiramisu Velvet: hot Tiramisu Velvet Latte, iced Tiramisu Velvet Latte and Tiramisu Velvet Cold Brew. The latte combines espresso, milk, vanilla and a mascarpone-style cold foam with cocoa on top. The cold brew version uses vanilla and a similar topping. In other words, the difference is the coffee base and temperature, not three entirely different dessert flavors.

For customers who prefer something fruit-forward, the new Apple Pomegranate Refresher pairs apple flavor with pomegranate-flavored popping pearls. The Snowy Apple version adds coconut milk; a lemonade variation is also offered. Starbucks says the Refresher platform may be prepared with no or light caffeine, or as an Energy Refresher. Because preparation changes caffeine and ingredients, customers should check the specific drink configuration in the Starbucks app or with the store instead of assuming every Apple Pomegranate version has the same content.

Which returning drinks can customers order?

Peppermint Mocha, Caramel Brulée Latte, Sugar Cookie Latte and Gingerbread Chai return in the 2026 season. Starbucks also highlights Iced Caramel Brulée Cream Chai, Iced Sugar Cookie Cream Matcha and several seasonal cold foams. Some products are espresso-based, others contain tea or are refreshers. The sweet flavor names alone do not identify caffeine content, allergens or the calorie count for a customized order.

A useful ordering strategy is to decide between espresso, cold brew, chai or a Refresher first, then compare size, milk, toppings and sweetness. Velvet Mascarpone and Caramel Brulée cold foams are not advertised as non-dairy options. Check the company's nutritional and allergen information if ingredients matter to you; the press release is a product announcement rather than a personalized nutrition guide.

What's new in Starbucks holiday food?

The menu includes a Bearista Cake Pop, Braised Short Rib Grilled Cheese and an updated Turkey & Stuffing Sandwich. The returning holiday bakery assortment includes the Cranberry Bliss Bar, Snowman Cookie and Sugar Plum Cheese Danish. Not every item will necessarily be available all day at every licensed, airport or grocery-store location. Store inventory, pricing, hours and app availability are local variables, not nationally fixed numbers in the announcement.

Red Cup Day 2026: November 17, not November 5

Starbucks lists November 17 for Red Cup Day. Its announcement says customers who purchase a qualifying handcrafted holiday beverage at participating U.S. coffeehouses receive a collectible reusable holiday cup, while supplies last. That is distinct from the new holiday paper cup designs used for regular drinks starting November 5. The holiday paper cup is not necessarily the promotional reusable cup.

Before making a special trip, check the official Starbucks holiday hub for qualifying drink terms, store participation and changes. A cup giveaway can run out without a chain-wide notice, so the promotion should never be described as guaranteed availability.

When do the Bearista cups arrive?

  • November 5: seasonal drinks, food and merchandise collections start rolling out.
  • November 10: surprise-box editions of the Glass Bearista cup are scheduled in limited quantities.
  • November 17: Red Cup Day reusable-cup promotion for eligible drinks at participating stores.
  • December 1: the original Bearista Glass Cold Cup returns to select coffeehouses, subject to stock.

This schedule helps explain why social posts showing different cups or launch dates may both refer to real Starbucks announcements. The company has multiple separate merchandise campaigns, not one universal release.

What the announcement does not confirm

The November 4 company story does not supply a single national price for every seasonal drink and collectible. It also does not promise a particular item will be available at your nearest store. Compare the location-specific menu before visiting, and distinguish official company disclosures from fan photos or resale listings.
