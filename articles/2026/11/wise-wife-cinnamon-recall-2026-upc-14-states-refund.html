Wise Wife Cinnamon Recall: UPC, 14 States and Refund

An FDA-posted October 29 recall affects a specific Wise Wife ground cinnamon package sold across 14 states. Here is how to identify it and request a refund.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 8, 2026 · Source-linked consumer recall reporting · Representative photograph

This is a source-linked desk explainer, not firsthand testing. AI-assisted drafting may be used. Check the linked FDA notice and our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photo of cinnamon, not the recalled Wise Wife product. Photographer: Ruby Sengar / Pexels.

Quick answer: SLR Food Distribution recalled Wise Wife Brand Ground Cinnamon on October 29, 2026, after Connecticut laboratory testing found elevated lead levels. Check the 110 g (3.88 oz) clear jar with a black screw cap, UPC 0688474302754, and best-before date April 25, 2027. Stop using a matching jar; the company says consumers can discard it or return it to the store for a full refund.

Which Wise Wife cinnamon is included in the 2026 recall?

The FDA-posted company recall dated October 29, 2026 identifies a 110-gram (3.88-ounce) clear plastic container with a black screw-top cap. The barcode is 0688474302754 on the back label; the best-before date is 4/25/2027. Match the product description, UPC and date rather than relying on the brand name alone.

This matters because an earlier, separate 2025 Wise Wife cinnamon recall concerned a smaller 50 g jar and a different UPC. The October 2026 notice is not a new recall of every cinnamon product or every Wise Wife jar.

Which 14 states received the recalled cinnamon?

The distributor reported retail and grocery distribution between September 8, 2025, and June 28, 2026 in Connecticut, Indiana, Kansas, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, North Carolina, Nebraska, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Texas, Virginia and Wisconsin. These are distribution states, not proof that every store in those states carried the item. A product may also have been moved after purchase, so check the label even if you live elsewhere.

How do shoppers get a refund?

  1. Locate the jar and compare the UPC, package size and best-before date with the FDA recall notice.
  2. Do not eat, taste-test or serve a matching product. Keep it away from children.
  3. The company instructs consumers to discard the product or return it to the place of purchase for a full refund. Ask the retailer about its return process before traveling.
  4. For questions, contact SLR Food Distribution at 1-516-437-3782, Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern, or slrusaorder@gmail.com, as listed in the official recall.

Why did the company recall this ground cinnamon?

The FDA notice says the Connecticut Agricultural Experiment Station collected and analyzed a sample that showed elevated lead levels. The company initiated a voluntary recall and instructed stores to stop sales. No illnesses had been reported at the time of that announcement. The recall notice does not provide a reliable basis to estimate any individual consumer's exposure or diagnose lead poisoning.

Lead exposure can be difficult to recognize because symptoms may be absent, particularly after smaller exposures. The FDA's broader cinnamon safety alert advises households to check their spice cabinets because cinnamon may be stored for long periods. If you suspect repeated exposure, especially involving a child or pregnancy, consult a qualified healthcare professional about whether testing is appropriate rather than assuming illness from a recall alone.
