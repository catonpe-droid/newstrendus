Gourmia Air Fryer Recall: Check GTF7530 and Get a Repair

A November 5 federal recall covers one Gourmia oven model sold at Target. Here's how to check the rear label and request the manufacturer's free doorstop repair kit.

Quick answer: The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced on November 5, 2026 that approximately 175,951 Gourmia Stainless Steel Digital 9-Slice Air Fryer Ovens are being recalled. The affected model is GTF7530, with specified date codes 3021 through 0126. Stop using an affected oven and request a free repair kit from Gourmia.

Which Gourmia air fryer ovens are recalled?

The federal notice identifies the Gourmia 9-Slice Digital Air Fryer Oven, model GTF7530, with UPC 810002862779. The affected units are stainless steel countertop ovens with two spring-loaded glass front doors and metal handles. The model number and date codes are printed on a label on the back of the appliance; the model and UPC can also be found on the original packaging.

The CPSC identifies date codes 3021 to 0126 on the affected units. Do not assume that every Gourmia air fryer is included. The model and label details matter more than appearance or a generic photograph.

How do I check my GTF7530 model and date code?

  1. Unplug the oven and let it cool completely before inspecting it.
  2. Look for the model GTF7530 on the label on the back of the unit.
  3. Compare the date code against the 3021–0126 range listed in the federal recall notice.
  4. If you still have the box, compare its UPC with 810002862779.
  5. Use the CPSC's official recall page and product photographs to confirm details. Contact Gourmia if a label is unclear.

These steps are for identification only. They do not constitute a safety inspection or a recommendation to test the oven while hot.

Why is the Gourmia oven being recalled?

According to the CPSC, the oven's spring-loaded doors can close unexpectedly when bumped while someone retrieves hot food. That can expose hands or wrists to a burn hazard. Gourmia received 57 reports of unexpected door closures, including 40 reported burn incidents involving consumers' hands or wrists. These are reported incidents, not an estimate of the chance of injury for an individual household.

Independent consumer coverage also described the November 5 recall and the burn reports; the Prevention report provides additional context. The federal notice remains the controlling source for the model and remedy.

How to get Gourmia's free repair kit

Stop using the recalled oven immediately. The CPSC directs owners to contact Gourmia for a free repair kit by mail. The kit contains an additional doorstop and installation instructions intended to prevent the doors from closing unexpectedly. This is a repair recall, not a blanket cash-refund program.

Visit Gourmia's recall information page or call 888-834-0092, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. (the federal notice does not specify a time zone). Have the model number and date code available. Follow the supplied instructions rather than improvising a door modification.

Where were the recalled ovens sold?

The CPSC says the affected ovens were sold at Target stores nationwide and Target.com from October 2021 through June 2026, for approximately $150. Buying an oven at Target does not by itself mean it is recalled; verify the model and date code. The federal recall number is 27-069.

Does this include other Gourmia appliances?

This November 5 recall concerns the GTF7530 air fryer oven. It is distinct from a separate February 2026 CPSC warning about Gourmia GPC625 pressure cookers, which involved a different hazard and remedy. Confusing the two could lead an owner to follow the wrong instructions.

What happens next for affected owners?

The recall was announced November 5, 2026. The safest next step for an owner with a matching label is to stop using the oven and obtain Gourmia's official kit. Check the manufacturer's recall page for any later updates to instructions.
