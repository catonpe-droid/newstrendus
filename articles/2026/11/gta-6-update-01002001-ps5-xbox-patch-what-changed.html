GTA 6 Update 01.002.001: PS5 Patch Size and What We Know

GTA 6 patch 01.002.001 appeared ahead of November 19. Reported PS5 and Xbox update sizes, missing patch notes and how to check downloads.

NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 18, 2026

At a glance: GTA 6 patch 01.002.001 appeared ahead of November 19. Reported PS5 and Xbox update sizes, missing patch notes and how to check downloads.

What is GTA 6 update 01.002.001?

Grand Theft Auto VI has received reported pre-launch patches as its November 19 release approaches. Forbes reported November 17 that update 01.002.001 began rolling out November 16, following update 01.002.000, which was reported November 13. This does not mean GTA VI had launched for general play early.

Rockstar had not released a detailed public changelog for those two updates in the reporting reviewed. That matters: claims that they added specific cars, fixed a named mission or changed graphics settings should be considered unverified until the developer publishes details.

What are the reported PS5 and Xbox patch sizes?

The Forbes report puts the PS5 update at 602.5 MB. It also cites an approximate 1.59 GB size for Xbox Series X|S, while explicitly warning that Rockstar has not confirmed the Xbox figure. Neither number represents the full GTA VI game installation size.

If your console shows a different download amount, first check which update it is applying. Game platforms may package updates differently. A photo of another player's installation screen is not an official universal storage requirement. Our GTA 6 file-size explainer discusses the pitfalls of viral storage claims.

When did PS5 owners receive the update?

According to reporting cited by Forbes, dataminers observed a three-wave PS5 rollout over November 16 and 17, with eligible preloaders covered by November 17. That is attributed reporting rather than a Rockstar-issued global release schedule. Xbox rollout timing remains less clear, with conflicting descriptions in the same coverage.

How do you install the patch before launch?

On PS5, highlight GTA VI and use the Options menu to check for updates. On Xbox Series X|S, look at the game's Manage Game and Add-ons menu, together with the console's Updates or Queue section. Let official downloads finish and check that the correct game edition is installed. Never install a patch file from an unrelated download site.

PS5 owners should also check the distinct system software 14.23.00 requirement reported ahead of launch. Updating firmware and updating GTA VI are two different procedures.

Is the update proof of launch problems?

Not by itself. Pre-release games often receive technical revisions, but the specific purpose of this patch has not been established by official notes in the sources reviewed. Its presence is not evidence of any particular defect, and it would be premature to promise a performance boost.
