X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillow Recall: NP001 Refund Steps

A November 5 federal recall covers a specific C-shaped nursing pillow sold online. Here is how to identify an unlabeled product and follow the refund process without confusing it with other feeding pillows.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 8, 2026 · Source-linked consumer safety reporting · Representative photograph

This is a source-linked desk explainer, not firsthand product testing. Research and drafting may use AI-assisted tools. See our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photograph of breastfeeding, not a photo of the recalled nursing pillow. Photographer: MART PRODUCTION / Pexels.

Quick answer: On November 5, 2026, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced the recall of about 19,596 X-VESOLJE nursing pillows, model NP001, sold by Kikea Store through Amazon. The pillows can obstruct an infant's breathing and do not meet mandatory safety standards. Stop using the affected pillow immediately and contact the seller to arrange a full refund. No incidents or injuries were reported in the federal notice.

Which X-VESOLJE nursing pillows are recalled?

The recall covers model NP001, a C-shaped nursing pillow sold in three designs: white with a leaves pattern; dark brown with multicolored feathers; and a multicolored puzzle-piece pattern. The federal notice says the pillows themselves have no markings or labels. That makes comparing their shape and fabric against the official CPSC photographs particularly important. Do not assume an unlabeled pillow is unaffected.

According to the CPSC's November 5 recall notice (27-071), the products were sold online at Amazon between April 2025 and June 2026 for approximately $24 to $34. These are historical sale prices, not refund amounts for individual customers. The seller is Shenzhen Yiyi Jiajia Trading Co., Ltd., operating as Kikea Store.

How to check a pillow without a model label

  1. Stop using the pillow while checking whether it matches the recall.
  2. Compare its C-shaped profile and the three listed patterns against the actual product photographs on the official recall page. The photograph accompanying this NewsTrendus article is illustrative and cannot be used for product identification.
  3. Review your Amazon order history for a Kikea Store or X-VESOLJE purchase from April 2025 through June 2026.
  4. If the design or order information suggests a match, contact the seller using the contact details in the CPSC notice for confirmation before destroying anything.

How to request the full refund

CPSC instructs owners to stop using the recalled pillow immediately and contact Kikea Store at zsally929@gmail.com for a full refund. The notice says the seller will ask consumers to cut the pillow in half after confirming the recalled product with the firm, then email a photograph of the destroyed pillow and dispose of it. Do not cut or discard the pillow before obtaining confirmation and understanding the required evidence. The notice does not specify a universal refund-processing time or a separate claims deadline; do not rely on an invented date.

For a current contact address or changes to the remedy, use the CPSC record rather than a social-media post or unofficial claims website. The CPSC identifies the remedy as a refund, not a replacement or repair kit.

Why does a nursing pillow present a suffocation hazard?

The CPSC says these pillows violate mandatory nursing-pillow standards because they can obstruct an infant's breathing. A product being sold for feeding support does not mean it is suitable for infant sleep. The recall is based on the hazard and standards violation; the absence of reported incidents at the time of announcement does not establish that continued use is safe.

Parents and caregivers should distinguish a nursing pillow from a safe infant sleep surface. The CPSC's safe-sleep resources explain the importance of a firm, flat sleep surface free of pillows and other soft items. This general safety guidance is not a substitute for following the product-specific recall.

What should Amazon buyers do now?

Check the order date and seller, compare the exact patterns on the government page, and keep your order record for reference. If the pillow matches, discontinue use and contact Kikea Store for the refund process. The federal recall applies to the identified model, not every nursing pillow sold on Amazon. No injuries had been reported as of the November 5 announcement; future updates should be checked against the official record.
