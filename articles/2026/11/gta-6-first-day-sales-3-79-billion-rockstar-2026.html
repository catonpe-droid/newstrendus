GTA 6 First-Day Sales: $3.79 Billion, What It Means

Rockstar reports $3.79 billion in GTA 6 first-day sales. See what the figure measures, how it compares with GTA 5 and what remains unverified.

NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 22, 2026

At a glance: Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI generated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales during its first 24 hours after its November 19, 2026 launch. That is a company-reported sales estimate, not an independently audited total. The game is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

How much did GTA 6 make on its first day?

Rockstar Games reported $3.79 billion in worldwide first-day retail sales for Grand Theft Auto VI. The announcement followed the game's November 19 launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reuters and Associated Press reported the figure as supplied by Rockstar, rather than a separately verified financial audit.

That distinction matters: first-day retail sales are not the same thing as Take-Two's net revenue, profit, cash received or long-term player spending. Refunds, platform fees, taxes and accounting recognition can affect how sales translate into financial statements. Readers should not interpret $3.79 billion as company earnings.

How does the GTA 6 launch compare with GTA 5?

Rockstar's figure would put the new title far ahead of the widely reported $1 billion earned by Grand Theft Auto V during its first three days in 2013. The comparison illustrates scale, but the periods are different: 24 hours for GTA VI versus 72 hours for GTA V. Pricing, digital distribution, preorder practices and the size of the gaming audience also changed substantially between launches.

AP reports that Rockstar also cited 32.5 million pre-launch copies sold. Preorders should not be added again to the reported first-day revenue as if they were an independent second sales stream; doing so risks double counting.

What do the figures tell buyers and investors?

For U.S. buyers, the relevant practical question is availability rather than a sales record. Rockstar's official GTA VI page identifies the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S editions. It does not establish a same-day native PC release. Compare the edition and storefront terms before buying; sales records do not establish whether any future feature will be included.

For investors following Take-Two Interactive, retail sales are an indicator of demand, not a substitute for the publisher's earnings releases. The final accounting treatment, platform economics, ongoing sales and any future online monetization remain separate questions. NewsTrendus does not make a share-price prediction from a launch-day headline.

What is confirmed, and what is still unknown?

Confirmed by company reporting: Rockstar announced the November 19 console release and reported a $3.79 billion worldwide first-day retail sales estimate. Not independently established by that announcement: final audited net revenue, profit margin, the precise PC launch date, or a separate GTA VI Online release date.

Readers tracking the multiplayer timetable can consult our GTA VI Online confirmation guide; for platform readiness and availability, see the GTA VI console launch guide. Both distinguish official announcements from speculation.

Where to verify the announcement

Check Rockstar's GTA VI Newswire for original product announcements and the AP report and Reuters report for independent journalistic context. The $3.79 billion figure remains explicitly attributed to Rockstar.
