X-VESOLJE Nursing Pillow Recall: NP001 Refund Steps

A federal recall covers nearly 19,600 nursing pillows sold online. Parents and caregivers should stop using the affected model and follow the seller's refund procedure.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 12, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

This source-linked explainer is not first-hand product testing. Research and drafting may use AI-assisted tools. See our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photograph of an unrelated household pillow, not the recalled nursing product. Photo: Marek Hrnčiarik / Pexels.

Quick answer: On November 5, 2026, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of approximately 19,596 X-VESOLJE nursing pillows, model NP001, because they fail the mandatory nursing-pillow safety standard and can obstruct an infant's breathing. Stop using the product and contact Kikea Store for a full refund. Read federal recall 27-071.

How to identify the recalled X-VESOLJE NP001 pillow

The affected pillow is C-shaped and was offered in three patterns: white with leaves, dark brown with multicolored feathers, and a multicolored puzzle-piece print. The model designation is NP001. Importantly, the CPSC says there are no markings or labels on the pillow itself. That means consumers should check their Amazon order history, product photographs and seller details rather than expect to find an NP001 label sewn into the fabric.

The pillows were sold on Amazon.com from April 2025 through June 2026, typically for $24–$34. The seller was Shenzhen Yiyi Jiajia Trading Co., Ltd., operating as Kikea Store. These dates and prices help identify a purchase but do not replace confirmation from the seller or CPSC.

What is the suffocation hazard?

According to the CPSC's November 5 notice, the recalled pillows violate the federal mandatory safety standard for nursing pillows because their design can obstruct an infant's breathing. This is a risk of serious injury or death, not merely a comfort or quality complaint. The agency reported no incidents or injuries at the time of the announcement. That does not make continued use safe.

A nursing pillow should never be treated as a sleep surface. The recalled model should not be used for feeding support while waiting for a refund. Families should follow current CPSC safe-sleep guidance for infant sleep arrangements; this article does not replace individualized medical advice.

How to request a full refund

First, stop using the pillow and keep it away from the baby. Contact Kikea Store at zsally929@gmail.com, the contact listed in the federal notice, and identify your purchase as the X-VESOLJE NP001 nursing pillow. Ask the seller to confirm the recall and refund instructions before destroying anything.

The CPSC says consumers will be asked to cut the pillow in half after confirming the recalled product with the firm, send a photograph of the destroyed item to the same email address, and then dispose of it. Do not send a photo of an intact product as proof of destruction or assume a retailer return alone satisfies the manufacturer's recall process. The announced remedy is a full refund, not a replacement pillow.

Does the recall cover every nursing pillow?

No. This specific recall covers the identified X-VESOLJE model and patterns, not all nursing pillows or all products sold on Amazon. Other brands may have separate notices. For example, the CPSC also issued a PILLANI nursing-pillow recall on October 29, with different model and batch identifiers. Check each notice individually instead of assuming the same refund contact applies.

What should caregivers do next?

Review the Amazon order record, compare the product's shape and print with the official CPSC photographs, and contact the seller if it matches. Keep copies of the purchase confirmation, refund correspondence and any requested disposal evidence. If you are unsure whether your pillow is covered, consult the official recall page rather than relying on social-media photos.
