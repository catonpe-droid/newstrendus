Veterans Day 2026: USPS, UPS and FedEx Delivery Hours

USPS closes post offices and pauses regular mail on November 11. UPS still picks up and delivers, while FedEx runs modified service. Here is what the carriers actually say and how to plan a shipment.

Quick answer: On Wednesday, November 11, 2026, all USPS post offices are closed and regular mail delivery is suspended. UPS says its pickup and delivery services are available, but two USPS-dependent services need an extra business day. FedEx lists modified service, not a blanket shutdown. USPS regular operations resume Thursday, November 12.

Is USPS open on Veterans Day 2026?

No. In its November 6 announcement, the U.S. Postal Service says all post office locations will be closed Wednesday, November 11, and that regular mail delivery and retail services resume Thursday, November 12. That is a nationwide holiday closure, not merely reduced counter hours. The USPS notice does not promise normal delivery of every expedited service on the holiday, so customers with time-sensitive Priority Mail Express shipments should confirm the service commitment for their particular label and destination rather than assuming delivery.

USPS's November 9 employee bulletin independently confirms the agency's observance. An AARP guide to 2026 postal holidays also notes that regular mail and packages are not delivered on the date.

Does UPS deliver on Veterans Day?

Yes, for its generally scheduled pickup and delivery operations. The UPS 2026 holiday calendar explicitly says November 11 pickup and delivery services are available and UPS Store locations are open. It also flags an important exception: UPS Ground Saver and UPS Mail Innovations shipments require one additional business day in transit because of the federal USPS holiday. These services can depend on USPS for part of delivery, so an otherwise open UPS network does not erase the postal closure.

The additional day is a service-level planning note, not a guaranteed arrival date for every package. Check the shipment's tracking details and your local store's posted hours before traveling.

Is FedEx open on November 11?

FedEx is operating with modifications. Its official 2026 holiday schedule lists FedEx service as “Modified service” for Veterans Day, while FedEx Office, FedEx Custom Critical and FedEx Logistics are listed as open. Its detailed calendar likewise lists FedEx Freight as open. Modified service may affect collection arrangements; the precise cutoff and available options depend on the service and location.

That is why “FedEx is open” is not enough for a last-minute package. Check the relevant local pickup cutoff and the package's tracking page, especially if using a drop box or requesting an on-call pickup.

What can you still do while post offices are closed?

USPS says customers can still use its online services for stamps, free shipping boxes, Hold Mail, Change of Address, PO Box rental or renewal and prepaid Click-N-Ship labels. Some post office lobbies have self-service kiosks for buying postage, weighing parcels and tracking shipments. Availability varies: a lobby kiosk does not mean a staffed counter is open or that a parcel will enter the regular delivery network on the holiday.

If you run a small online store, distinguish label creation from carrier acceptance. Review delivery promises for USPS-bound orders and the UPS services that explicitly add a business day; avoid promising a Thursday arrival solely because a label was printed Wednesday. For an urgent FedEx shipment, verify a location-specific cutoff before leaving home.

What happens next, and when is the next major closure?

USPS states that regular delivery and retail operations resume Thursday, November 12. The next major shared holiday is Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26: the UPS schedule lists no pickup or delivery that day, and the FedEx schedule lists standard FedEx service closed. Check service-specific exceptions and local arrangements.
