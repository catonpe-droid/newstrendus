Coachella 2027 Lineup, Dates and Ticket Sale Guide

The new lineup is confirmed, but festivalgoers need to distinguish sold-out general admission passes from the shuttle-inclusive tickets being offered in the November sale.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 11, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

Quick answer: Coachella 2027 runs April 9–11 and April 16–18 at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA headline the three nights; Daddy Yankee has a special top-billed appearance. The festival lists a November 13 sale for remaining shuttle-inclusive passes, while regular general admission is marked sold out on its official ticket page.

Who is headlining Coachella 2027?

Organizers have named Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA as the three main headliners. Daddy Yankee receives a separate special billing. The newly revealed lineup also includes Madonna, Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy, JISOO, Kehlani, Don Toliver, Dom Dolla, Robyn, Tiësto and others. The Associated Press's November 11 report independently confirms the three headliners, dates and venue; Pitchfork's lineup coverage adds historical context about the artists' prior festival appearances.

The distinction matters: Daddy Yankee is prominently billed for a special appearance, but the announced nightly headliners are Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA. Readers should consult the festival's official website for the complete artist-by-day lineup and any later changes rather than assuming every named performer appears each night.

What are the dates and where is the festival?

Weekend One takes place Friday, April 9 through Sunday, April 11, 2027. Weekend Two runs Friday, April 16 through Sunday, April 18, 2027. Both are scheduled at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, in California's Coachella Valley. Coachella's official 2027 pass information confirms the dates and identifies ticket options separately for each weekend.

For out-of-town attendees, those dates are the practical starting point for booking lodging, transport and time off. The two weekends are distinct ticket purchases; buying a pass for one weekend does not automatically grant admission to the other.

When do Coachella 2027 tickets go on sale?

Remaining ticket options are scheduled to go on sale Friday, November 13, 2026, at noon Pacific time (3 p.m. Eastern), according to reporting by TheWrap and the festival's official pass listings. That is the sale time, not a guarantee that every ticket type will be available.

Importantly, the official pass page currently marks standard general admission passes sold out. It lists a GA + three-day shuttle pass at $779, Tier 2, for each weekend, with a November 13 sale date. The site says service fees are included in displayed pass prices and tiers differ by price, not admission privileges. Availability can change without notice; check the live checkout before making plans.

What should buyers check before paying?

First select the correct weekend. Then verify whether the listing is standard GA, GA with shuttle, VIP, or a lodging bundle. A shuttle-inclusive pass is not identical to a bare GA ticket, and camping or hotels are separate arrangements unless a package explicitly says otherwise. The official website provides pass and add-on details as well as waitlist options for some products.

Do not assume a quoted resale price or third-party listing is an official festival price. Use the organizer's direct site to confirm what remains on sale, fees, fulfillment conditions and refund or transfer terms before entering payment details. A sellout indicator for one type of pass does not necessarily mean all festival admission options are unavailable.

Will both weekends have the same artists?

Coachella's official advance-sale guidance describes the two weekends as featuring the same lineup, art, food and activities. Individual performance times, surprise appearances and operational arrangements can still vary. The detailed daily schedule and set times should be treated as unconfirmed until organizers release them closer to April.

What happens next?

The immediate milestone is the November 13 ticket sale. After that, watch official Coachella announcements for pass availability, the complete daily schedule, travel instructions and any lineup revisions. As of this November 11 report, no performance set times have been independently confirmed here. This guide will not present speculative schedules as established facts.

Sources and verification: Coachella official 2027 passes, Coachella purchase guidance, Associated Press (Nov. 11), Pitchfork (Nov. 11) and TheWrap (Nov. 10). Ticket availability is a point-in-time observation and may change.
