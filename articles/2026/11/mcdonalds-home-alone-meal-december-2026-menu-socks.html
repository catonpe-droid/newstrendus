McDonald's Home Alone Meal: Dec. 1 Menu and Socks

McDonald's will introduce a limited-time Home Alone Meal at participating US restaurants on December 1, 2026. Customers can choose a breakfast, lunch or dinner entrée, and each meal includes one of four collectible sock designs. The movie-themed pizza fries and dipping sauce are for lunch and dinner; the Everything Sundae McFlurry is sold separately.

Quick answer: The Home Alone Meal launches December 1 at participating US McDonald's locations while supplies last, according to McDonald's official announcement. The company has not specified a nationwide fixed price or final availability date. Check your local restaurant or app on launch day.

When does the McDonald's Home Alone Meal come out?

The announced US launch is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, at participating restaurants nationwide. McDonald's describes the promotion as limited-time and subject to supplies. That does not guarantee every restaurant will have every item or sock design throughout the promotion. The official announcement is dated November 17, and Good Morning America independently confirmed the December 1 date.

What is included at breakfast, lunch and dinner?

The meal lets customers choose an entrée, with the company giving the Sausage Egg and Cheese McGriddles as a breakfast example and the Big Mac as a later-day example. Lunch and dinner versions feature medium Little Nero's Pizza McShaker Fries and Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce. The fries use pizza-inspired seasoning with tomato, garlic, herbs and cheese flavors; the sauce draws on tomato and Parmesan flavors. The announcement does not say the special fries are included at breakfast.

The Everything Sundae McFlurry combines vanilla soft serve, hot fudge, Oreo pieces and M&M's. It is a separate limited-time purchase, not a guaranteed component of the meal. USA Today reporting also distinguishes the separately sold dessert from the bundled meal.

Which collectible socks come with the meal?

Each meal comes with one of four designs themed around Kevin McCallister or McDonald's characters Grimace, Birdie and the Hamburglar. Designs are collectible, but the company has not guaranteed customers a choice of all four at every location. Availability depends on participating restaurants and remaining supplies. The meal is a food promotion with a collectible accessory, not a sale of the movie or a movie-ticket offer.

How much does the Home Alone Meal cost?

McDonald's November 17 national announcement did not give a single US price. Do not treat prices circulating on social media as official nationwide pricing. Because restaurant participation, entrée choices and local pricing can differ, customers should check the McDonald's app or the menu at their intended restaurant beginning December 1. Compare the price shown at checkout before placing an order.

What else is happening for the Home Alone promotion?

The campaign features Macaulay Culkin reprising Kevin McCallister in an advertisement. McDonald's also announced a Home Alone-themed Los Angeles pop-up for December 4–5. App-based promotions and streaming offers may have separate eligibility requirements; read their official terms rather than assuming they are automatically included with a meal purchase.

What should customers check on December 1?

First confirm your location is participating, then check whether your preferred entrée and special fries are offered at that time of day. Ask which sock designs remain available and verify the price before paying. If you specifically want the Everything Sundae McFlurry, remember it is sold separately. For related restaurant coverage, see McDonald's US sales and value-menu reporting and the site's Business news section.

Sources: McDonald's announcement, November 17, 2026; ABC Good Morning America, November 17; USA Today syndicated reporting, November 17. Prices and local inventory are not confirmed nationally.
