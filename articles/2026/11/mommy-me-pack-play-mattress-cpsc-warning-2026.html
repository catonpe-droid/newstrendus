Mommy & Me Play Yard Mattress Warning: Check Model

A November 12 federal safety warning covers approximately 13,278 Mommy & Me Pack & Play aftermarket mattresses. Parents should check model MNM-PNPMAT-WHT and the play-yard compatibility exception before using the mattress.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 16, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

Quick answer: The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) says consumers should immediately stop using the Mommy & Me Pack & Play aftermarket mattress, model MNM-PNPMAT-WHT, in incompatible play yards. The warning does not apply when this mattress is used in the compatible Mommy & Me Play Yard model MNM-PMP-BLCK. No company recall or refund program was announced in the CPSC warning.

Which Mommy & Me mattress is affected?

The November 12, 2026 CPSC warning identifies model MNM-PNPMAT-WHT, a white aftermarket mattress measuring approximately 38 by 26 inches. The brand and model number appear on the mattress label, either embroidered or printed. These mattresses were sold separately from play yards, including through Amazon.com between July and September 2025 for around $36; the agency says other third-party sellers or sites may also have offered them.

The CPSC estimates approximately 13,278 units. It reports no incidents or injuries associated with this warning. That does not remove the potential hazard: the warning concerns a possible dangerous fit, not a confirmed injury count.

Why does the CPSC say the mattress is dangerous?

An aftermarket mattress that is too small for a particular play yard can leave a gap along the side. An infant's face could become trapped between the mattress and the play-yard wall, creating an entrapment and suffocation risk. The CPSC says the product violates the federal crib-mattress standard because compatibility with certain play yards has not been adequately established.

Federal rules require aftermarket mattresses to be tested and proven compatible with the specific play-yard or non-full-size crib models for which they are intended. A mattress that looks close in size is not necessarily safe to substitute.

What is the model exception?

The CPSC explicitly excludes use in the compatible Mommy & Me Play Yard, model MNM-PMP-BLCK, from this warning. Consumers should verify both model numbers rather than assume that any Mommy & Me product, or any similarly sized play yard, qualifies. If the play yard is a different model, the CPSC directs consumers to stop using the warned-about aftermarket mattress.

What should parents do now?

First, check the sewn or printed mattress label for MNM-PNPMAT-WHT. Next, identify the exact play-yard model using its manufacturer label or manual. If the combination is covered by the warning, stop using the aftermarket mattress immediately. The agency instructs consumers to dispose of the affected mattress and not sell or give it away. Check the official warning for product-identification information and any subsequent updates.

The manufacturer, Mommy & Me LLC of Cheyenne, Wyoming, had not agreed to a recall or an acceptable consumer remedy when the warning was issued, according to the CPSC. Do not assume a refund is available. The CPSC's warning is a stop-use notice, not a company-initiated recall.

How can families choose a safer replacement?

The American Academy of Pediatrics' safe-sleep guidance advises using a firm, flat mattress specifically designed for the crib or play yard, with a snug fit and fitted sheet only. Keep pillows, loose blankets, toys and extra padding out of an infant's sleep space. Do not add a second mattress to compensate for a gap. For replacement parts, follow the play-yard manufacturer's instructions and confirm model compatibility.

What happens next?

The CPSC warning, numbered 27-081, was issued November 12. As of that notice, the agency reported no injuries and no agreed manufacturer remedy. Consumers should monitor the official CPSC notice for changes rather than rely on a retailer listing alone.
