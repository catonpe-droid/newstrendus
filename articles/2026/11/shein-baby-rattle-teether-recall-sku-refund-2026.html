SHEIN Baby Rattle Recall: SKU and Refund Steps

A new U.S. recall identifies one specific seven-piece baby toy set sold through SHEIN. The packaging SKU—not just the appearance—is the clearest way for families to check their purchase.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 12, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

This is an original source-linked consumer explainer, not firsthand product testing. AI-assisted drafting may be used; see our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photograph of an unrelated rattle during family playtime, not the recalled SHEIN product. Photo: William Fortunato / Pexels.

Quick answer: On November 12, 2026, SHEIN Distribution Corporation recalled approximately 2,657 seven-piece baby rattle and teether toy sets because detachable purple and orange knobs pose a choking risk. The affected packaging carries SKU sk25070936134444119. Parents and caregivers should stop using the set, keep it away from children and contact SHEIN for a refund. Read CPSC recall 27-083.

Which SHEIN baby rattle and teether set is affected?

The federal Consumer Product Safety Commission notice describes a set of seven pastel-colored toys in different shapes. Look for SKU sk25070936134444119 on the upper-right corner of the product packaging label. A toy that merely resembles the recalled set should not be assumed to be included or excluded: check the SKU and the official product photographs. The recall identifies the importer as SHEIN Distribution Corporation of Los Angeles and the country of manufacture as China.

What is the choking hazard, and were injuries reported?

According to CPSC, the purple and orange knobs can separate from toys intended for children under three, violating the federal small-parts ban. A detached component can create a serious choking hazard. No incidents or injuries were reported in the November 12 recall notice. That does not mean the affected toys are safe to keep using; the agency explicitly directs consumers to stop use immediately.

Where and when was the recalled set sold?

The affected product was sold online at US.Shein.com from November 2025 through July 2026 for approximately $10. The stated recall quantity is about 2,657 units. Families who bought other SHEIN teething products should compare their packaging with the specific SKU and images rather than assuming that every rattle or teether from the retailer is recalled. CPSC's separate earlier spiral-toy recall concerns a different product and identifying code.

How to get the SHEIN baby toy recall refund

First, take the affected set away from children and stop using it. Next, use SHEIN's official product-recall page or contact the company to request a refund. CPSC says customers will be asked to affirm that they disposed of the recalled toy set to receive the refund. Follow the company's instructions; do not sell or donate the recalled toys. For recall support, the CPSC lists 833-853-8668, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific Time, and uscsteam@shein.com. NewsTrendus does not handle refund claims or collect customer details.

What should parents check next?

Inspect packaging labels, compare the seven-piece set with the official recall images, and retain purchase records if available. If the packaging is missing, contact SHEIN using the official recall channel rather than relying on an unverified social-media image.
