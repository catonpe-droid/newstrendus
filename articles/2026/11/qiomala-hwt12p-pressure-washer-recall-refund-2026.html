QIOMALA Pressure Washer Recall: HWT12P Refund Steps

A November 12 federal recall covers one specific QIOMALA electric pressure washer model. The model label and purchase period are the fastest ways to check whether yours is included.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 14, 2026 · Source-linked desk reporting · Representative photograph

This is a source-linked consumer explainer, not first-hand product testing. AI-assisted drafting may be used. Read our editorial and corrections policy.

Representative licensed photograph showing pressure washing, not the recalled QIOMALA product or a reported incident. Photo: Erik Mclean / Pexels.

Quick answer: The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall on November 12, 2026, covering 80 QIOMALA HWT12P electric pressure washers because an inadequately long power cord can encourage extension-cord use and create shock or electrocution hazards. Stop using an affected machine and contact QIOMALA for a full refund. Check the official CPSC notice (recall 27-084).

Which QIOMALA pressure washers are recalled?

The recall is for QIOMALA model HWT12P, a 1.2-gallon-per-minute electric pressure washer sold in green-and-black or blue-and-black. CPSC describes a machine about 14 inches deep, 11 inches wide and 15 inches high, supplied with a 500 ml soap tank, four quick-disconnect nozzles and a 20-foot hose. The HWT12P model number is on a label on the back, near where the power cord enters. Check that label against the official product photographs; the color alone does not establish whether a washer is included.

Where and when was model HWT12P sold?

According to the federal recall, the affected units were sold online through Amazon.com from April through June 2026 for approximately $130. CPSC identifies Ningbo Miyao E-Commerce Co., doing business as QIOMALA, as the importer. The recall covers about 80 units, not every electric pressure washer sold on Amazon. If you cannot find the label or are unsure of the model, compare the unit with the agency's images before following any destruction instructions.

What is the electrical hazard? Have injuries been reported?

The federal notice says the power cord is not adequately long, which may encourage users to connect an extension cord. In a wet pressure-washing environment, that creates a potentially serious electrical shock or electrocution risk. No incidents or injuries had been reported in the November 12 notice. The absence of reported injuries does not override CPSC's instruction to stop using the recalled machine immediately. This recall notice specifically identifies the cord-length hazard; do not assume it describes a different model or a separate ground-fault-interrupter recall.

How to request the QIOMALA recall refund

First, stop using the affected washer and unplug it. Contact QIOMALA at QIOMALA@163.com for a full refund and follow the company's instructions. CPSC says the refund process requires a photograph showing the model number and the pressure washer rendered unusable by cutting its power cord in half. Never cut a cord while the device is plugged in; confirm the recall and refund requirements with the company before destroying the product. Dispose of the disabled machine in accordance with local rules. NewsTrendus does not administer refunds or request customer details.

What should buyers do next?

Review your Amazon order history for purchases made in April, May or June 2026, inspect the label near the cord entry point and use the CPSC recall 27-084 page as the authoritative reference. Keep purchase information available for the refund conversation. Other consumer recalls have different products and remedies; for example, our Sharper Image fire pit recall guide concerns a separate November 12 safety announcement, while our Huffy electric trike recall guide concerns a repair rather than this refund. Do not apply those instructions to QIOMALA.
