Veterans Day 2026 Free Meals: ID, Hours and Restrictions

Veterans and eligible service members can claim selected restaurant meals on Wednesday, November 11. The key differences are proof-of-service rules, time windows, participating locations and whether the offer is dine-in only.

Quick answer: Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Applebee's offers a free entrée from a limited menu to eligible military guests at participating U.S. locations; Denny's offers an Original Grand Slam from 5 a.m. to noon. Many deals require identification and dining in. Check the individual location before traveling.

Which Veterans Day 2026 meals have confirmed terms?

The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs' continuously updated 2026 offer list provides a starting point, but explicitly warns that offers can change and participation differs by location. The following examples are not an exhaustive list and should not be treated as guarantees at every franchise.

What ID do veterans need for a free meal?

Bring evidence of military service, rather than assuming a uniform or verbal statement will be accepted. Applebee's November 2 announcement specifically accepts military or retired-service ID, a current leave-and-earnings statement, qualifying veterans-organization cards, a DD214 and several other forms of proof. Other chains can set their own requirements. Confirm what the restaurant will accept before visiting.

What time do offers start and end?

There is no universal Veterans Day free-meal schedule. The VA lists Denny's Original Grand Slam from 5 a.m. to noon, whereas Applebee's offer applies to participating restaurants on November 11 during their applicable operating hours. Some promotions involve vouchers for later use instead of a free meal on the spot. Check your local restaurant's hours, whether reservations or dine-in are required, and whether tax, drinks or gratuity remain payable.

Does Applebee's also provide a $5 return card?

Yes, subject to separate restrictions. Its official 2026 announcement says eligible dine-in guests receive a $5 bounce-back promotional card for a later order. The card expires November 29, 2026 and cannot be redeemed on the day it is issued. It is not cash and has additional limits; read the official terms.

How to avoid a wasted trip on November 11

First check the VA's dated offer list. Then use the restaurant's official location page or call the specific branch to confirm participation, menu choices and service hours. Bring the requested ID, allow for lines and remember that free entrées may exclude drinks, taxes and tips. Family members are not automatically eligible unless an individual offer says so.
