Boshengya Rug Recall: Identify Rugs and Claim a Refund

Boshengya recalls 83 shag rugs sold on Amazon over a fire risk. Check pink and orange rugs, sizes, packaging labels and the official refund process.

By NewsTrendus Editorial Desk · November 16, 2026 (U.S. Eastern) · Independently written from credited sources · Representative editorial photograph

This is a source-linked desk explainer, not first-hand reporting. Research and drafting may use AI-assisted tools. Follow the cited source links and read our editorial and corrections policy.

At a glance: The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission announced a recall of 83 Boshengya shag area rugs on November 12, 2026, because they fail a federal flammability standard. Buyers should stop using affected rugs and contact the seller for a full refund.

Which Boshengya rugs are recalled?

The official CPSC recall notice (No. 27-088) identifies pink or orange Boshengya shag area rugs, approximately five or six feet wide and eight feet long. The packaging bears the words “INCREDIBLY SOFT.” The notice says the products were sold through Amazon.com from September through October 2026, priced between $30 and $65. The affected count is 83 rugs. Not every shag rug or every Amazon rug is part of this recall; match the brand, color, dimensions, label and purchase history before treating a product as affected.

Why are these rugs unsafe?

CPSC says the recalled rugs violate the mandatory U.S. flammability standard for carpets and rugs, creating a fire hazard that could cause serious injury or death. The agency reports no incidents or injuries associated with this recall as of its November 12 announcement. That does not remove the risk: the recall is based on the product's failure to meet a safety requirement, not on a confirmed household fire. The announcement does not identify a specific ignition incident or claim that every rug will catch fire.

How do owners obtain a full refund?

Stop using the rug immediately. The official remedy is a full refund through Boshengya, contacted at boshengya666@outlook.com. CPSC says consumers will be asked to cut the recalled rug in half, email a photograph of the destroyed product to that address and then dispose of it. Contact the recall provider and confirm the instructions before destroying anything; do not keep using the rug while waiting. The notice does not promise an automatic Amazon refund or specify a processing time, so avoid relying on unverified claims about payout dates.

What should Amazon buyers check?

Review Amazon order history for purchases in September or October 2026, then compare the item against CPSC's product photographs and packaging description. If the product appears to match, follow the official contact instructions. If the brand, size or color is unclear, ask the recall contact for identification guidance. The CPSC notice includes pictures of the actual pink and orange recalled rugs; the photo accompanying this article is representative only and must not be used to identify a recalled item.

What does the recall mean for other household products?

This announcement is limited to the specified Boshengya rugs. Consumers can search the CPSC recall database for other safety notices rather than assuming unrelated carpets are covered. NewsTrendus also tracks distinct consumer safety actions, including the QIOMALA pressure washer refund recall and the Kakgold toy recall; these involve different products and hazards.
